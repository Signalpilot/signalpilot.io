> SPREAD:
> → Difference between bid and ask
> → You pay it on every trade
> → Wider during low liquidity
> → Tighter on major pairs/assets
>
> SLIPPAGE:
> → Difference between expected and filled price
> → More common in fast markets
> → Worse with larger positions
> → Can be positive or negative
>
> HOW TO MINIMIZE:
> → Trade liquid assets
> → Avoid major news releases
> → Use limit orders when possible
> → Account for costs in R:R
>
> REALITY CHECK:
> → A scalping strategy with 5 pip targets
> → 2 pip spread + 1 pip slippage = 3 pips cost
> → You need 8 pips gross to net 5
>
> Factor costs into your edge calculation.

>
> WHAT IS R:R?
> → Potential profit vs. potential loss
> → 1:2 = risk 1 to make 2
> → 1:3 = risk 1 to make 3
>
> THE MATH:
> → 1:1 R:R needs >50% win rate to profit

> → 1:3 R:R needs >25% win rate to profit
>
> WHY IT MATTERS:
> → You can be wrong more than right
