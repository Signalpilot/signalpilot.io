Slide 4 — Rules
THE RULES
Backtest Honestly
1
BE HONEST
No cherry-picking winners. Log every setup that matches your criteria — wins and losses.
2
INCLUDE COSTS
Commissions, fees, and slippage eat into your edge. Account for them or your results lie.
3
TEST ACROSS CONDITIONS
Trending, ranging, volatile. Your strategy must survive all market regimes — not just the easy one.
