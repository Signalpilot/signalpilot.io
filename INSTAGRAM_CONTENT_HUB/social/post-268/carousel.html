The Basics

What is Signal Pilot?

Q What exactly is Signal Pilot?
Signal Pilot is a suite of 7 professional trading indicators built for TradingView, paired with a free 82-lesson education hub. We provide analysis tools and education — not signals, predictions, or financial advice.
Q Do I need trading experience?
No. The education hub starts from absolute zero — candlestick basics, risk fundamentals, psychology. Our 82 lessons progress from beginner through professional level. The indicators complement your learning at every stage.
Key Point
Signal Pilot is education-first. The indicators enhance your analysis. The education teaches you why, not just what.

The Indicators

Do They Repaint?

Q Do your indicators repaint?
Zero repainting. Ever. Once a bar closes, the signal is final. No retroactive changes, no disappearing arrows, no history revision. What you see is what happened.
Q Do they give buy/sell signals?
No. Signal Pilot provides analysis frameworks, not trade signals. Our indicators show market conditions — momentum, regime, cycles, structure — so you can make your own informed decisions.
Why This Matters
Repainting indicators create false confidence. They look perfect in hindsight but fail in real-time. Our tools show you reality, not fantasy.
