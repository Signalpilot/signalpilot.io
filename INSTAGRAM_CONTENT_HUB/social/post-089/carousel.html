Slide 4 — How to Identify
04 / 06
How to Identify a Zone
1
Find the Impulse
Look for a strong, aggressive move away from an area with large-body candles. The bigger the impulse, the stronger the zone.
IMPULSE STRENGTHCRITICAL
2
Mark the Base
The consolidation or single candle before the impulse is your zone boundary. This is where the orders originated.
BASE CLARITYHIGH
3
Draw the Zone
Extend a rectangle from the base candle's high to its low. Use the body for conservative zones, wicks for aggressive zones.
ZONE PRECISIONHIGH
4
Wait for Retest
Trade the zone only when price returns to it with confirmation. Never anticipate — let price come to you.
PATIENCE REQUIREDESSENTIAL
