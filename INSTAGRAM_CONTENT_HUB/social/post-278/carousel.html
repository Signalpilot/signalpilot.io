The Foundation

Markets Have Three States

↗
Uptrend
Higher highs and higher lows. Buyers in control.
↘
Downtrend
Lower highs and lower lows. Sellers in control.
↔
Sideways
Price bouncing between levels. No clear control.
Key Insight
70% of a trend’s move happens in 30% of its time. Most of the trend is spent in consolidation. The big moves come in sudden bursts.

Confirmation

5 Pieces of Trend Evidence

1
Price Structure
HH/HL for up, LH/LL for down. The most basic and reliable evidence.
2
Moving Averages
Price above the 20/50/200 EMA stack = bullish. Below = bearish.
3
Volume
Trend moves should have increasing volume. Pullbacks should have decreasing volume.
4
Momentum
Oscillators trending with price confirm direction. Divergence warns of exhaustion.
Evidence #5: Multi-Timeframe
The trend on the higher timeframe matters most. A 15-minute uptrend inside a daily downtrend is a correction, not a reversal.

Avoid These

4 Trend Identification Mistakes

1
Fighting the Higher TF
Fix: Always check D1 direction first
2
Calling Reversals Too Early
Fix: Wait for structure break, not just one candle
3
Ignoring Consolidation
Fix: Sideways is a valid answer. Don’t force a trend
4
One Indicator Only
Fix: Use multiple evidence types for confirmation
Remember
The trend is your friend until the structure breaks. Don’t anticipate the break — wait for it. Patience pays.
