Slide 2 — Definition
What is OTE?

Optimal Trade Entry

The zone between 62% - 79% Fibonacci retracement where the probability of a successful trade is highest while maintaining an excellent risk-to-reward ratio.

It's the Goldilocks zone — not too shallow, not too deep.

Slide 3 — Deep Enough
Why 62-79%
📍

Deep Enough

Entry closer to your stop loss = smaller risk

Better risk-to-reward ratio on every trade

More room for profit before resistance

Shallower entries (38-50%) leave money on the table

The deeper the entry, the better the R:R — up to a point.

Slide 4 — Not Too Deep
Why 62-79%
⚠️

Not Too Deep

Trend structure still intact at this level

Higher probability of continuation

Beyond 79% often signals trend failure

Deep entries (85%+) = lower win rate

Too deep and you're fighting a potentially broken trend.

Slide 5 — Implementation
Implementation

How to Use OTE

1 Identify a clear impulse move (swing high/low)
2 Draw Fibonacci retracement on the impulse
3 Mark the 62-79% zone as your OTE area
4 Wait for price action confirmation in zone
5 Place stop below the impulse low/high
