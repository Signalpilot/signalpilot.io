The Ghost
Signals

A trader backtested a free indicator. Win rate: 87%. His hands trembled with excitement.
He went live the next morning. By noon, three signals had vanished from his chart.
The signals he’d tested against never existed in real time. They were ghosts — painted after the fact.
He asked the developer: “Why did the signal move?”

“It recalculated. That’s normal.”

“Normal? I placed a trade based on a signal that no longer exists.”
