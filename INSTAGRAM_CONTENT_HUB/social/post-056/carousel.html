THE TRUTH
Doji ≠ Reversal
✗
COMMON BELIEF
Doji means price is about to reverse. See doji, enter counter-trend.
=
REALITY
Doji means indecision. Neither bulls nor bears won that period. The battle ended in a draw.
→
IMPLICATION
The doji itself tells you nothing about direction. What happens next determines who won.

DOJI TYPES
The Four Variations
✝
Standard Doji
Open equals close. Equal wicks. Pure indecision at any location.
NEUTRAL
⊤
Dragonfly
Long lower wick, no upper wick. Buyers rejected lower prices strongly.
BULLISH LEAN
⊥
Gravestone
Long upper wick, no lower wick. Sellers rejected higher prices.
BEARISH LEAN
+

THE FRAMEWORK
Reading a Doji Correctly
1
Identify the Doji
Spot the candle where open ≈ close. Note the wick lengths and symmetry.
2
Check the Location
A doji at key support or resistance matters. Mid-trend dojis are noise.
3
Read the Context
After a strong trend? That is when indecision carries weight. In a range? Ignore it.
4
Wait for Confirmation
The next candle decides. A strong follow-through candle validates the signal.
5
Define Your Plan
Entry after confirmation candle. Stop beyond the doji wick. Target at next structure.
COMMON TRAP
Trading the doji alone without waiting for the next candle. Doji shows a pause, not a decision. If you are guessing, step away.

THE TRUTH
What the Doji Really Teaches
Indecision, Not Reversal
A doji is a pause. The market has not decided. You should not decide either.
Location Is Everything
The same doji means nothing mid-range but everything at a key level.
Confirmation Required
Never trade a doji alone. Wait for the next candle to tell you who won.
Context Over Pattern
A pattern without context is decoration. Read the story the chart is telling.

"The market rewards patience — not pattern recognition without context."
