Slide 2 — What Is It
ANATOMY

What Is a Doji?

Upper wick — bulls pushed up
Open ≈ Close — tiny or no body
Lower wick — bears pushed down

Neither bulls nor bears won. A doji signals indecision, not reversal.

Slide 3 — Doji Types
VARIATIONS

Types of Doji

Standard
Equal wicks, tiny body
Long-Legged
Extended wicks, high volatility
Dragonfly
Long lower wick, bullish bias
Gravestone
Long upper wick, bearish bias

Decides

BULLISH CONFIRM
Doji → strong green
= buyers took control
BEARISH CONFIRM
Doji → strong red
= sellers took control
The doji asks the question. The next candle gives the answer.

👀
At Key S/R Level
