Slide 2 — What Is It
ANATOMY

What Is a Doji?

Upper Wick
Bulls pushed price up
Open ≈ Close
Tiny or no body — indecision
Lower Wick
Bears pushed price down

Neither bulls nor bears won. A doji signals indecision, not reversal.
