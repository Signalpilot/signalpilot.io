THE TRUTH
What Confluence Really Teaches
Stack, Don't Guess
One signal suggests. Three signals stacked at the same level — that is where probability lives.
Obvious Over Forced
If you have to convince yourself, it is not confluence. Real alignment jumps off the chart.
Independent Factors
Each signal must be independently derived. Overlapping derivatives do not count as true confluence.
Patience Pays
Wait for the zone. Wait for the stack. The best trades come to those who refuse to force them.

"The market rewards those who wait for alignment — not those who chase every signal alone."
