Slide 5 — Survival Framework
05 / 08
SURVIVAL RULES

How to Stay
in the Game

01
Position size that doesn’t hurt
If losing the trade would change your behavior, your mood, or your next trade — you’re risking too much. Size down until the loss is boring.
02
Stop losses you actually honor
A stop loss only works if you let it execute. Moving it, widening it, or removing it turns a planned loss into an unplanned disaster.
03
Max daily drawdown limit
Lose 2% in a day? Close the charts. Walk away. The market will be there tomorrow. Your capital might not be if you keep going.
04
No revenge trades. Ever.
The urge to “make it back right now” is the single most destructive force in trading. Take the loss. Reset. Come back with a plan, not a grudge.

YOUR CHECKLIST

The Survivor’s
Checklist

  • ✓ Capital preservation > Capital growth
  • ✓ Staying in the game > One big win
  • ✓ Consistent small gains > Occasional home runs
  • ✓ Every dollar you protect is a dollar that compounds
  • ✓ Every dollar you lose needs to be earned back — plus the growth you missed
