Slide 4 — Why Comparison Hurts

Why Comparison
Hurts

The psychological toll of measuring yourself against others.

▲
Creates Unrealistic Expectations You start chasing returns that don't match your experience level or risk profile.
⚠
Encourages Excessive Risk Trying to match someone else's gains pushes you into positions you cannot afford.
▼
Kills Your Confidence Every win feels small. Every loss feels personal. Your self-belief erodes trade by trade.
✕
Distracts From Your Journey Time spent watching others is time stolen from refining your own edge.
