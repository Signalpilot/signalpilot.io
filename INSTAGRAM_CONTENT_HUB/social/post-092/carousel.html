R:R Rules to Live By
1
Set R:R Before Entry
Know your stop and target before the trade. Never enter blind.
DISCIPLINECRITICAL
2
Minimum 1:2 R:R
If the setup doesn't offer at least 1:2, skip it. Better setups will come.
MIN THRESHOLD1:2
3
Never Widen Your Stop
Moving your stop to avoid getting hit destroys your R:R. Accept the loss.
LOSS ACCEPTANCEESSENTIAL
4
Let Winners Run
Cutting winners short kills your reward side. Use partials and trailing stops.

Slide 5 — Key Truth

◆
R:R Is Your Real Edge.
★
Win rate means nothing without R:R context. A 40% win rate at 1:3 outperforms 60% at 1:1.
✓
Always define risk before defining reward. Your stop comes first, then your target.
↑
The best traders lose more often than they win. Their edge is in the asymmetry of payoff.
◆
R:R is the single most important concept in risk management. Master it or stay unprofitable.

It's not about being right. It's about being paid when you are.
