Slide 4 — Rules
R:R Rules to Live By
1
SET R:R BEFORE ENTRY
Know your stop and target before the trade. Never enter blind.
2
MINIMUM 1:2 R:R
If the setup doesn't offer at least 1:2, skip it. Better setups will come.
3
NEVER WIDEN YOUR STOP
Moving your stop to avoid getting hit destroys your R:R. Accept the loss.
4
LET WINNERS RUN
Cutting winners short kills your reward side. Use partials and trails.

Slide 5 — Key Truth
R:R Is Your Real Edge.
Win rate means nothing without R:R context
A 40% win rate at 1:3 R:R outperforms 60% at 1:1
Always define risk before defining reward
The best traders lose more often than they win

It's not about being right. It's about being paid when you are.
