A long green candle followed by a small-bodied candle that gaps up, then a long red candle that closes below the midpoint of the first candle. Which pattern is this?
A Morning Star
B Evening Star
C Three White Soldiers
D Bullish Engulfing
Answer: B — Evening Star is a bearish reversal pattern that forms after an uptrend and signals potential downside movement.
Slide 8 — Summary
Summary

Key
Takeaways

1 Single-candle patterns like Doji, Hammer, and Shooting Star reveal immediate sentiment shifts at key levels.
2 Multi-candle patterns such as Engulfing, Morning Star, and Evening Star provide stronger confirmation signals.
3 Context matters — always consider the prevailing trend, support and resistance levels, and volume when reading candlestick formations.
4 Never trade patterns in isolation — combine candlestick analysis with indicators, price action, and risk management for higher probability setups.
