The Wisdom of Alignment

You cannot force alignment.
You can only recognise it.
Wait patiently. Trade confidently.
But never forget — even alignment
is not certainty. Only confluence.
From the Signal Pilot Chronicle
The Paradox
Alignment is the strongest signal the Seven can produce — yet it still carries no guarantee. The chronicle teaches that confluence is not certainty. It is the highest form of probability, tempered by discipline and the humility to know that markets owe nothing.
Lesson
Disagreement is normal.
Alignment is exceptional.
Respect both equally.
