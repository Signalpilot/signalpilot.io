Slide 4 — Why Comparison Fails
04 / 08

Why Comparing
Fails in Trading

✕
Different Capital A $500 account and a $50K account cannot play the same game. Your size dictates your rules.
✕
Different Goals Some trade for income, some for growth, some to learn. Comparing results across different goals is meaningless.
✕
Different Risk Tolerance Their 50% gain might come with drawdowns you'd never survive. The risk behind the return is invisible.

You're comparing your Chapter 3
to someone else's Chapter 30.
