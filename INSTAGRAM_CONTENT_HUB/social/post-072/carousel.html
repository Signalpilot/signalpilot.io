Slide 4 — Framework
04 / 06
THE FRAMEWORK
How Your Journal
Becomes Your Edge
1
Log Every Trade
Not just the winners. Every trade. The losses and the scratches contain the most valuable data.
2
Review Weekly
Set aside 30 minutes each weekend. Read your entries. Look for repeating patterns — good and bad.
3
Spot Behavioral Patterns
You'll notice things: 'I always lose on Mondays' or 'I size up after wins.' These are your leaks.
4
Adjust Your Process
Once you see the pattern, fix it. Remove the leak. Double down on what works.
5
Compound Your Edge
Each cycle of log → review → adjust makes you marginally better. Over months, this compounds into a real edge.
COMMON TRAP
Only journaling winners. Your losing trades contain the most valuable data. If you only track wins, you only see half the picture. The losses reveal your leaks — and leaks are what you fix to improve.
