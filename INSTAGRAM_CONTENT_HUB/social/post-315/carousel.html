Slide 7 — How to Read Level Strength

How to Read It

1
Check the Touch Count
Look at how many times price has tested the level. Three or more touches signal genuine interest from the market.
2
Evaluate Recency
Prioritize levels that were tested in the last few sessions. Stale, untouched levels lose relevance over time.
3
Compare Timeframes
A level visible on the weekly chart carries more weight than one that only appears on a 15-minute chart.
4
Gauge the Reaction
Sharp, decisive bounces confirm strength. Slow, grinding moves through a level reveal weakness in that zone.
