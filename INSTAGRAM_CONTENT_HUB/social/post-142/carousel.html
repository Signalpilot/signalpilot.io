CONCEPT

What Is
Correlation?

The degree to which two assets move together. Measured from -1 (perfect inverse) through 0 (independent) to +1 (perfect lockstep).

−1.0 0 +1.0
POSITIVE
+1
Assets move in the same direction together
ZERO
0
Assets move independently of each other
NEGATIVE
−1
Assets move in opposite directions

Slide 5 — The Hidden Danger
05 / 08
THE HIDDEN DANGER

Doubling Risk,
Not Diversifying

You go long ETH and long SOL thinking you’re diversified. Both are 0.9+ correlated to BTC. BTC drops 5% — both positions lose.

Intended Risk
2%
+ Corr. Position
4%
Actual Exposure
~7.5%
RULE
If two assets have correlation above 0.7, treat them as one trade for risk management. Your total exposure across correlated positions should stay within your risk limit.

Slide 7 — Regime Changes
07 / 08
CRITICAL WARNING

When Correlations
Break

Correlations that held for months can invert overnight during regime changes.

01
Fed Policy Shifts
Rate decisions can decouple BTC from stocks overnight
02
Black Swan Events
Panic selling makes “everything correlates to 1”
03
Liquidity Crises
Gold and DXY may suddenly move together when cash is king
04
Narrative Shifts
When BTC becomes “digital gold,” its correlations change
ALWAYS
Reassess correlations after major macro events. Past relationships are not guarantees of future behavior.
