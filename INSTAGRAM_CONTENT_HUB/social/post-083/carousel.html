The 4 Phases

Phase 1 Accumulation Smart money quietly buys at low prices. Range-bound, low volume.
Phase 2 Markup Price breaks out. Trend begins. Public starts to notice.
Phase 3 Distribution Smart money sells to late buyers. Range-bound at highs.
Phase 4 Markdown Price falls. Panic selling. Cycle prepares to repeat.
ACC → MKP → DST → MKD → ACC

Slide 3 — How to Spot

How to Spot Each Phase

Accumulation: Tight range + declining volume
Price stops falling but doesn't rally. Quiet absorption.
Markup: Higher highs + rising volume
Clean breakout from the range. Trend confirmation.
Distribution: Wide swings + heavy volume
Euphoria at tops. Smart money offloads positions.
Markdown: Lower lows + panic volume
Support breaks. Fear takes over. Price accelerates down.

Why It Matters

Trade with smart money, not against it.

Most retail traders buy during distribution and sell during accumulation. Wyckoff teaches you to recognize where you are in the cycle before entering a trade.

Know the phase. Know the bias. Know the edge.
