Slide 2 — Four Phases

The 4 Phases

Every market cycle follows the same Wyckoff sequence. Learn to read it.

A
Accumulation Smart money quietly buys at low prices. Range-bound with declining volume. The stealth phase.
Volume: Low · Price: Flat
M
Markup Price breaks out. Trend begins. Public starts to notice and chase higher highs.
Volume: Rising · Price: Trending
D
Distribution Smart money sells to late buyers. Range-bound at highs with euphoric sentiment.
Volume: Heavy · Price: Choppy
K
Markdown Price falls sharply. Panic selling. Capitulation. Cycle prepares to repeat.
Volume: Panic · Price: Falling
ACC → MKP → DST → MKD → ACC

Slide 3 — How to Spot

How to Spot Each Phase

Volume and price action together reveal the current Wyckoff phase.

A
Accumulation: Tight Range + Declining Volume Price stops falling but doesn't rally. Quiet absorption by institutions.
Volatility: 25% — Stealth buying
M
Markup: Higher Highs + Rising Volume Clean breakout from the range. Trend confirmation with momentum.
Momentum: 65% — Trend phase
D
Distribution: Wide Swings + Heavy Volume Euphoria at tops. Smart money offloads positions to retail.
Risk: 88% — Exit zone
K
Markdown: Lower Lows + Panic Volume Support breaks. Fear takes over. Price accelerates downward.
Fear: 95% — Capitulation
