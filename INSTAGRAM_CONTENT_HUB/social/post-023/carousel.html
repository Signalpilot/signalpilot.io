The Problem

Same Chart, Different Intent

A sideways range looks identical whether smart money is quietly buying or quietly selling.

Price alone won't tell you. You need volume and context.

SIGNALPILOT
03 / 08
Accumulation
Smart money buying quietly after a downtrend.
→ Happens after extended declines
→ Institutions absorb supply
→ Retail thinks bottom is in
→ Leads to MARKUP phase
SIGNALPILOT
04 / 08
Signs of Accumulation
→ Volume decreases on dips
→ Springs (false breakdowns) common
→ Higher lows form within range
→ Selling pressure exhausts
Result
Breakout to the upside
SIGNALPILOT
05 / 08
Distribution
Smart money selling into strength after an uptrend.
→ Happens after extended rallies
→ Institutions offload to retail
→ Retail thinks it's just a pause
→ Leads to DECLINE phase
SIGNALPILOT
06 / 08
Signs of Distribution
→ Volume increases on rallies
→ Upthrusts (false breakouts) common
→ Lower highs form within range
→ Buying pressure exhausts
Result
Breakdown to the downside
