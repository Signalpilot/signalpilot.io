Uptrend

HIGHER HIGHS

HIGHER LOWS
Each pullback holds above the prior low. Demand is strong enough to catch price earlier each time.

KEY INSIGHT
When highs get higher and lows get higher, the trend is structurally bullish. The most fundamental pattern in trend identification.

PATTERN 2
Downtrend
L
LOWER HIGHS
Each rally fails to reach the previous peak. Sellers are stepping in earlier with every swing.
H
LOWER LOWS
Each decline breaks through the prior floor. Selling pressure intensifies with each wave.
SWING 1 — NEW LOW

DOWNTREND CONFIRMED
CAUTION
When highs get lower and lows get lower, the trend is structurally bearish. Do not try to buy the dip in a confirmed downtrend.

PATTERN 3
Range-Bound
R
Resistance
Price rejected at the ceiling. Rally fails to make new highs — sellers defend.
CEILING
S
Support
Price bounces from the floor. Decline fails to make new lows — buyers defend.
FLOOR
N
No Trend
No HH/HL or LH/LL pattern. Price chops sideways between levels.
CHOPPY
W
Wait for Break
Trade range edges with tight stops, or wait for a decisive breakout.
PATIENCE
REMEMBER
No clear structure means no clear edge. Wait for a breakout or trade the range edges with strict risk management.

05 / 06
T
THE TRUTH
What Trend Identification
Really Teaches
Structure First
Before indicators, before signals — read the structure. HH/HL or LH/LL tells you everything.
Bias, Not Entry
The trend gives direction, not a specific entry. Combine with levels for timing.
No Trend = No Trade
When structure is unclear, the edge disappears. The best traders sit on their hands.
Timeframe Matters
A downtrend on 5m can be an uptrend on daily. Always align with the higher timeframe.

"The trend is your friend — until the structure breaks."
