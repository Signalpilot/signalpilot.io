Slide 2 — The Truth

Confirmation Bias

Your brain is wired to seek information that confirms what you already believe.

And to ignore information that contradicts it.

The chart doesn't lie. But your brain does.

SIGNAL PILOT
Slide 3 — Example

Example: You're Long BTC

What You See
"Golden cross forming!"
"RSI cooling off!"
"Support holding!"
What You Ignore
Downtrend on daily
Weak volume
Resistance overhead

You didn't analyze. You justified.

Slide 4 — Consequence

The Consequence

Red Flags Ignored
Warning signs were screaming at you. You didn't see them because you didn't want to.

Slide 5 — How It Works

How It Works

Once you have a position, your brain becomes a lawyer, not a scientist.

A scientist seeks truth.
A lawyer seeks evidence for their side.

You're in court, not a lab.

Slide 6 — The Cost

The Cost

Confirmation bias makes you:

→ Double down on bad ideas
→ Hold losers too long
→ Miss obvious exit signals
→ Convince yourself "this time is different"

The Fix

Key Practice
Before every trade, actively look for reasons you're WRONG.

Be a scientist, not a lawyer.
