Conservative — small positions, tight stops, capital preservation above all

Moderate — balanced risk-reward, systematic sizing, steady growth

Your Analysis Style

How you read the market defines how you think.

☰
Approach Technical Analysis

Charts, patterns, indicators. Price action tells the story. You trust what the chart shows over what the news says.

⚙
Approach Fundamental Analysis

Earnings, macro data, economic cycles. You trade the why behind the move, not just the move itself.

Many traders blend both. The key: what makes sense to you?
