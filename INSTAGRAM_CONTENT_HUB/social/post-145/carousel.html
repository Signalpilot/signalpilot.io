Slide 4 — What IGN Is NOT
04 / 07
MISCONCEPTIONS

What IGN
Is NOT

×NOT AN ENTRY SIGNAL
IGN does not tell you to buy or sell right now. It tells you momentum is igniting.
Use IGN as context, not as a trigger. Pair it with structure for entries.
×NOT A GUARANTEE
Breakouts can fail. IGN detects conviction, not certainty.
Always use stops. IGN improves odds, it doesn’t eliminate risk.
×NOT A SIMPLE CROSSOVER
Unlike MA crossovers, IGN requires five independent factors to align simultaneously.
This is why IGN fires less often — but when it does, it means something.
REMEMBER
Fewer signals, higher quality. That’s the Pentarch philosophy.
