THE MAGIC

Confluence
Scoring

One signal is a guess. Multiple systems aligned is a trade. OmniDeck scores how many of its 10 systems agree — giving you a single, actionable read.

Aligned
7+ systems agree. High conviction zone. This is when you act.
Partial
4–6 systems agree. Proceed with caution. Reduce size.
Conflict
Systems disagree. Stay out. No trade is a valid trade.
