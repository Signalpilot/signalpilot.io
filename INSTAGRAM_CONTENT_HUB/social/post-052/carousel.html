Slide 2 — Support
CONCEPT 1

Support

F
FLOOR LEVEL
A price area where buying interest appeared. Demand is strong enough to stop the decline and push price back up.
B
BUYER DEFENSE
Each time price touches this level, buyers step in — creating a repeating bounce pattern that confirms the zone.
PRICE DROPS TOWARD LEVEL
TOUCHES SUPPORT — BUYERS ACTIVE
BOUNCE — DEMAND WINS
▼
SUPPORT LEVEL CONFIRMED
KEY INSIGHT
More touches = stronger level. A support tested 3+ times carries significantly more weight than a single bounce.
THE FLOOR

Slide 3 — Resistance
CONCEPT 2

Resistance

C
CEILING LEVEL
A price area where selling interest appeared. Supply overwhelms demand and pushes price back down.
S
SELLER PRESSURE
Each time price touches this level, sellers step in — creating a repeating rejection pattern that confirms the zone.
PRICE RISES TOWARD LEVEL
HITS RESISTANCE — SELLERS ACTIVE
REJECTION — SUPPLY WINS
▼
RESISTANCE LEVEL CONFIRMED
KEY INSIGHT
The more times price is rejected at a level, the stronger that resistance becomes. Repeated rejections = serious supply zone.
THE CEILING

The Flip

Support breaks → becomes resistance

WAS
Support
→
BECOMES
Resistance
When buyers can no longer hold a level, it means sellers took control. That old floor now becomes a ceiling.
Watch for: Price breaking below support, then retesting it from underneath and getting rejected.
