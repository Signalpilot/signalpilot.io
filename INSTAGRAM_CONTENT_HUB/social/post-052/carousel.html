Resistance

Slide 7 — Truth
S
THE TRUTH

What S/R Really Teaches

Zones, Not Lines
Support and resistance are areas of interest, not exact prices. Think in ranges, not decimals.
Memory Over Math
These levels work because traders remember them. Market psychology creates the floor and ceiling.
Breaks Are Signals
When a level finally breaks, it tells you the balance of power shifted. Adapt or get trapped.
Context Is King
A support level on the daily chart matters more than one on the 5-minute. Always check the bigger picture.
