TRIPLE FORMATIONS
Soldiers & Crows
Bullish Reversal
Three White Soldiers
Three consecutive bullish candles, each closing higher with small upper wicks. Shows sustained buying pressure and strong momentum building.
Best after a downtrend at support — watch for exhaustion on the third candle
Bearish Reversal
Three Black Crows
Three consecutive bearish candles, each closing lower with small lower wicks. Sellers are in complete control with relentless distribution.
Best after an uptrend at resistance — confirms distribution and exhaustion of buyers
💡
Mirror patterns. Soldiers and Crows are exact inverses. Both require three strong-bodied candles with small wicks. The smaller the wicks, the stronger the conviction.

REJECTION PATTERNS
Tweezer Tops & Bottoms
Bearish Reversal
Tweezer Top
Two candles with matching highs — first bullish, second bearish. The market tested a level twice and was rejected both times. Sellers won.
Strongest at established resistance after extended uptrend
Bullish Reversal
Tweezer Bottom
Two candles with matching lows — first bearish, second bullish. Double rejection at the same low shows buyers defending a price floor with conviction.
Strongest at established support after extended downtrend
Precision Matters
Tweezer highs/lows don't need to be exact to the tick — within a few ticks is valid. The key is the double rejection at the same level showing institutional interest.

RARE REVERSALS
Stars & Abandoned Babies
Rare Bullish Reversal
Abandoned Baby
Gap down + Doji + Gap up. The doji is isolated by gaps on both sides, "abandoned" by the trend. Extremely rare but one of the most powerful reversal signals in all of technical analysis.
Bullish Reversal
Morning Star
Bearish candle + small indecision candle (star) + bullish candle. Signals the dawn of a new uptrend. The star shows sellers are losing control before buyers take over.
Bearish Reversal
Evening Star
Bullish candle + small indecision star + bearish candle. The mirror of Morning Star — signals dusk for the uptrend. Buyers exhaust, sellers step in decisively.

CONTEXT RULES
A Pattern Without
Context Is Noise
📍
Location
Patterns at key support/resistance are 3x more reliable than patterns in no-man's land. Where matters more than what.
📊
Volume
Volume should confirm the pattern. Soldiers with rising volume = strong. Soldiers with declining volume = suspect exhaustion.
📈
Trend Context
Reversal patterns need a trend to reverse. Three White Soldiers in a sideways market means nothing. Trend first, pattern second.
⏱️
Timeframe
Daily chart patterns are far more reliable than 5-minute chart patterns. Higher timeframe = fewer false signals and bigger moves.
🔗
Confluence
Pattern + level + volume + trend = high probability. A pattern alone is a coin flip. Stack confirmations to build a real edge.
🎯
Follow-Through
The candle after the pattern is the real test. If the next candle doesn't follow through, the pattern has failed. Cut quickly.
The #1 Mistake
Trading patterns in isolation without checking context. A beautiful Three White Soldiers formation into major resistance is actually a short setup, not a long one. Always zoom out.
