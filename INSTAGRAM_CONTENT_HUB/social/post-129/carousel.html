THE FOUR PILLARS
Core Management Techniques
↗
Scaling In
Enter with partial size. Add more as the trade confirms your thesis. Reduces initial risk exposure while building into strength.
↘
Scaling Out
Take partial profits at predetermined targets. Let the remaining runner ride. Locks in gains while keeping upside potential alive.
⇡
Moving Stops
Trail your stop as the trade moves in your favor. Protect accumulated profits. Give the trade room to breathe without giving back gains.
◆
Adding to Winners
Pyramid into positions showing strength. Never add to a losing trade. Let the market confirm your thesis before committing more capital.

MOVING STOPS
Trail & Protect Profits
📐
ATR-Based Stop
Place initial stop 1 ATR below signal bar. Structured, not arbitrary. Gives the trade room to breathe.
🔒
Breakeven Move
After first partial profit, move stop to entry price. Eliminates downside risk entirely on remaining position.
📈
Swing Trail
Move stop behind each new higher low (longs) or lower high (shorts). Follows market structure, not emotions.
⚡
Momentum Exit
Tighten stop when momentum fades. Volume dries up or candles shrink? Protect what you've got.
🎯
Target Zones
Pre-define 1R, 2R, 3R targets. Take partials at each level. Never let a winner turn into a loser.
🚫
Never Widen Stops
Moving stop further away from entry = adding risk. Stops only move in your favor, never against you.
