Slide 3 — Pillars
Framework
5 Pillars of Discipline
1
Define Your Rules
Write entry, exit, and risk rules before the market opens — never trade without a written plan
2
Respect Your Stop Loss
A stop loss is a commitment, not a suggestion — honor it without exception every time
3
Size Correctly
Risk the same percentage on every trade — never let emotion inflate your position size
4
Journal Everything
Record every trade, every emotion, every deviation — accountability is the foundation
5
Walk Away When Done
Hit your daily target or max loss? Close the charts — overtrading is indiscipline
