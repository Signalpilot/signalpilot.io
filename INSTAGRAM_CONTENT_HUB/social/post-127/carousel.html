Slide 3 — The Hunt

THE MECHANICS

How Stops
Get Hunted

Price sweeps the obvious level → hits your stop → reverses → goes your direction without you.

WHY IT HAPPENS
Institutional players need liquidity to fill large orders. Clustered retail stops provide exactly that. Your stop loss is their entry.

Slide 4 — The Fix
04 / 07
THE FIX

Smart Stop
Placement

1
Add Buffer Below Obvious Levels
Don’t place your stop exactly at support. Add 1–2 ATR beyond the obvious zone to survive the sweep.
2
Use ATR for Volatility-Based Stops
An ATR-based stop adapts to current market conditions. Tight stops in volatile markets get destroyed.
3
Place Beyond the Liquidity Grab Zone
Think in terms of structure, not distance. Put your stop where the thesis is truly invalidated.
4
Accept Slightly Worse R:R
A wider stop with higher probability beats a tight stop that gets hunted every time. Survival trumps precision.

Slide 5 — Mindset

THE SHIFT

Think Like the
Predator

“Before placing a stop, ask yourself: would I put my stop here if I were trying to hunt someone else’s? If yes, move it.”
— THE PREDATOR TEST

Better stops aren’t about wider stops. They’re about smarter placement. That one shift changes everything.

THINK DIFFERENTLY
Stop asking “where should my stop go?” Start asking “where would price need to go to prove me wrong?” The answer is rarely at the obvious level.
