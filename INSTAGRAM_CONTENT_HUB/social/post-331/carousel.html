Getting Started

Setup in 3 Steps

1
Download TradingView
Get the free app from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in with your existing TradingView account.
2
Open a Chart
Search for any ticker. Tap the indicators icon (fx) at the top of the chart screen.
3
Add Signal Pilot
Search "Signal Pilot" under Invite-Only scripts. Tap to add any of the 7 indicators to your chart.

Best Practices

Smart Mobile Habits

Use Larger Timeframes
Stick to 1H, 4H, or Daily charts on mobile. Lower timeframes are hard to read on small screens.
Set Alerts, Not Trades
Use mobile for monitoring and alerts. Execute trades from desktop when possible for precision.
Landscape Mode
Rotate your phone for a wider view. TradingView supports full-screen landscape charting.

Settings for Mobile

Know the Limits

Mobile Caveats

Limitations
Smaller screen means less context. Drawing tools are harder to use. Pine Script editing is not available on mobile. Multi-monitor layouts don't translate to phone screens.
Workarounds
Focus on key levels only. Use alerts to track price action instead of watching charts. Save complex analysis for desktop — use mobile for monitoring and quick checks.
