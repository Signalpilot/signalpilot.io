Slide 3 — How to Find

How to Find Them

1
Identify Displacement
Look for a sharp, aggressive candle or series — a strong impulsive move that shows conviction.
2
Find the Last Opposing Candle
The candle immediately before the move, in the opposite direction. This is the order block.
3
Mark the Candle Body as a Zone
The open-to-close range becomes your order block zone. This is where institutions placed orders.
4
Wait for Price to Return
Order blocks act as magnets — price often retests before continuing in the direction of the move.

Slide 4 — Validation
04 / 05

What Makes an OB Valid

⚡
Created a Structure Break
The move from the OB must have caused a BOS or CHoCH to confirm strength.
▭
Left a Fair Value Gap
An FVG behind the move shows true displacement and institutional involvement.
☢
Swept Liquidity
The move should have taken out resting stop losses or triggered pending orders.
◆
HTF Alignment
The order block should align with higher timeframe bias for maximum confluence.
✓
Not Yet Mitigated
Price hasn't returned to fill the zone yet — unmitigated OBs hold the strongest reactions.

Not every candle is an order block. Context is everything.
