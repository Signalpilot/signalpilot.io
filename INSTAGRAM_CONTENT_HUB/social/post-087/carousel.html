Two Types of Order Blocks

Bullish OB
The last bearish candle before a strong bullish move. Smart money was buying here.
Bearish OB
The last bullish candle before a strong bearish move. Smart money was selling here.

Slide 3 — How to Find

How to Find Them

1
Identify a strong impulsive move
Look for displacement — a sharp, aggressive candle or series
2
Find the last opposing candle
The candle immediately before the move in the opposite direction
3
Mark the candle body as a zone
The open-to-close range becomes your order block zone
4
Wait for price to return to it
Order blocks act as magnets — price often retests before continuing

What Makes an OB Valid

Created a structure break (BOS or CHoCH)
Left a Fair Value Gap behind
Caused liquidity to be swept
Aligns with higher timeframe bias
Has not been fully mitigated yet

Not every candle is an order block. Context is everything.
