Slide 7 — Mistakes
07 / 08
COMMON MISTAKES

What Goes
Wrong

×
Bottom-Up Analysis
Starting on the 5-minute chart and trying to find direction. If the daily is bearish, that 5m long setup is fighting the current.
×
Forcing Confluence
When only 1 of 3 timeframes agrees, skipping the other two and taking the trade anyway. No confluence = no trade.
×
Too Many Timeframes
Analyzing 7 timeframes creates analysis paralysis. Stick to exactly 3: direction, structure, entry.
×
Ignoring the Higher TF
The higher timeframe defines your stop level. Better entries from lower TFs mean tighter stops and bigger R multiples.
REMEMBER
The higher timeframe always wins. A perfect 5-minute setup inside a daily downtrend is a losing trade waiting to happen.
