Slide 5 — What We Don’t Do
05 / 07

What We
Don’t Do

Knowing what we refuse to do is just as important as knowing what we build. These are our red lines.

✕
Lock Basic Education Behind Paywalls
Foundational trading knowledge should be accessible to everyone. We will never gate the basics behind a subscription or one-time fee.
✕
Create Dependency on Signals
Our goal is to make you an independent trader. If you cannot trade without us, we have failed. Signals support learning, not replace it.
✕
Overpromise Results
No guaranteed returns. No “quit your job in 30 days” nonsense. Trading is hard, and we will always be honest about that reality.
✕
Use Pressure Sales Tactics
No countdown timers, fake scarcity, or manipulative urgency. If our tools are good, they sell themselves. No pressure needed.
