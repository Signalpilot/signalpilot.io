MARKET EVOLUTION

4 Things That Always Change

🌊
Volatility Cycles
Markets alternate between compression and expansion. The same stop-loss width that works in low-vol dies in high-vol.
📊
Trend Character
Trending markets become ranging. Ranging markets explode. Your bias from last month is likely wrong today.
💱
Liquidity Patterns
Where stops cluster changes as market structure evolves. Yesterday's liquidity pool is today's fair value gap.
⏰
Session Behavior
The best entry window shifts with macro events, interest rates, and institutional positioning. Routines need updating.

TWO PATHS

Rigid vs. Adaptive

Rigid Trader
Uses one strategy in all conditions
Same position size regardless of vol
Blames the market when it stops working
Refuses to adjust timeframes
Dies slowly over 6-12 months
Adaptive Trader
Has strategies for trending + ranging
Scales size with market conditions
Reviews and adjusts monthly
Switches TF when structure changes
Survives and compounds for years

TRADER EVOLUTION

The Adaptation Timeline

Month 1-3
Find What Works
Test strategies on demo. Find your edge. Document what conditions it thrives in and when it fails.
Month 3-6
Identify Weak Spots
Your first drawdown reveals gaps. Was it the strategy or the conditions? Learn to tell the difference.
Month 6-12
Add a Second Mode
Develop a ranging strategy to complement your trending one. Or vice versa. Two modes doubles your opportunity window.
Year 2+
Read the Room First
Start every session by assessing conditions before looking for setups. Context first, entries second. This is mastery.

FRAMEWORK

The Monthly Adaptation Review

Monthly Check-In Protocol
5 Questions Every 30 Days
01 Conditions: Is the market trending, ranging, or transitioning? Has volatility expanded or compressed vs. last month?
02 Win rate shift: Has my win rate changed by more than 10%? If so, is it the strategy or the conditions?
03 Best setups: Which 2-3 setups performed best? Double down on those. Cut the rest.
04 Position sizing: Should I scale up (confidence + low vol) or scale down (uncertainty + high vol)?
05 Timeframe fit: Is my primary timeframe still showing clean structure? Or should I move up/down one TF?
