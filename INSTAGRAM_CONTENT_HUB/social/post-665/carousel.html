Slide 3 — Three Confirmation Filters
THE SOLUTION

Three Confirmation Filters

V
VOLUME SURGE
Breakout on declining volume is suspect. Real breakouts have volume expansion — conviction from both sides of the market.
R
RETEST
Price breaks, pulls back, holds the level as new support. The retest confirms the breakout was real — not a trap.
M
MULTI-TIMEFRAME
Breakout on 1H confirmed by 4H direction. Daily for direction, 4H for timing, 1H for precision. Alignment is everything.
CONFLUENCE
All 3 filters aligned = high-probability setup. Fewer confirmations = skip the trade. Patience is the edge.

Slide 5 — The Truth

THE TRUTH

What Breakouts Really Teach

Volume Confirms
Real breakouts are accompanied by volume expansion. No volume = no conviction. Always check the tape.
Patience Pays
Wait for the retest. The best entries come after confirmation, not during the initial break. Let the trap play out.
Risk Management
Never risk more than 1–2% per trade. Position sizing is your survival tool. Protect capital above all.
Multi-Timeframe
Daily for direction. 4H for timing. 1H for precision. Always check the bigger picture before entering.
