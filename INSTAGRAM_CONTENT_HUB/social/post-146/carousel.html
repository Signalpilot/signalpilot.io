Slide 2 — Definition

CONCEPT

What Is
Drawdown?

The decline from your equity’s highest point to its lowest point before recovery. It measures the worst losing streak your account has endured.

KEY METRIC
Max drawdown is the single most important number for evaluating any trading strategy. A 60% win rate means nothing if the drawdown wipes you out first.

Slide 3 — Recovery Math
03 / 08
THE MATH PROBLEM

Asymmetric
Recovery

Losses and gains are not symmetrical. The deeper the hole, the exponentially harder the climb back.

−10%
→ +11%
−20%
→ +25%
−50%
→ +100%
−75%
→ +300%
CRITICAL
At 50% drawdown, you need to double your remaining capital just to break even. This is why capital preservation is the first rule of trading.

Slide 4 — The Spiral
04 / 08
THE REVENGE SPIRAL

How 15%
Becomes 30%

1
Down 15% on the month
Frustration builds. “I need to make this back.”
2
Double position size
Trade bigger to “recover faster.” Emotions override the plan.
3
Bigger losses compound
Larger size × emotional decisions = accelerated drawdown.
4
15% becomes 30%
Now you need +43% to recover instead of +18%. The spiral wins.
THE FIX
When down, cut size in half. A smaller position lets you trade without desperation clouding every decision.

Slide 5 — Circuit Breakers
05 / 08
PROTECTION SYSTEM

Circuit
Breakers

Pre-set rules that force you to stop before emotion takes over. No exceptions.

−3%
Daily Max Loss
Hit 3% daily loss → done for the day. Close charts. The market will be there tomorrow.
−7%
Weekly Max Loss
Hit 7% weekly loss → reduce size by 50% for remainder of the week. Review journal before resuming.
−15%
Monthly Circuit Breaker
Hit 15% monthly loss → mandatory 3-day break. Full strategy review required before live trading resumes.
NON-NEGOTIABLE
Set these limits before you start trading. You cannot make rational decisions about when to stop while you’re losing.

SURVIVAL TRUTHS

Myths vs
Reality

MYTH
“I’ll trade bigger to recover faster”
REALITY
Bigger size during drawdown accelerates losses exponentially
MYTH
“Drawdowns mean my strategy is broken”
REALITY
Every strategy has drawdowns. It’s the depth and duration that matter
MYTH
“Good traders don’t have losing streaks”
REALITY
Good traders survive losing streaks because they manage size and emotion
MYTH
“Taking a break is giving up”
REALITY
Taking a break is protecting your capital for when the edge returns
TRUTH
Drawdowns are inevitable. Blow-ups are optional. The difference is management.
