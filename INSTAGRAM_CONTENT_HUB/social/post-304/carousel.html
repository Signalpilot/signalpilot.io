In Practice

What Patience Actually Looks Like

01
Pre-Session Prep
Mark your levels before the market opens. Know where you’ll act before price gets there.
02
Wait for Confluence
One signal is interest. Two signals is attention. Three signals is action. Not before.
03
Journal While Waiting
Review yesterday’s trades. Study the chart. Prepare levels for the next session.
04
Accept the Miss
If the setup moved without you, it wasn’t your trade. The next one will come. They always do.

Patience isn’t staring at a screen doing nothing — it’s doing everything except pressing the button.
