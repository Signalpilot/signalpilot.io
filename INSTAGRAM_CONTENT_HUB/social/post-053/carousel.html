Trading Emotions
Revenge trading after losses. FOMO buying at tops. Fear selling at bottoms. Emotions drive, logic dies.

Trade setups, not feelings.

Wanting profits without putting in work. No time for lessons, plenty of time for losses.

Education before execution.
