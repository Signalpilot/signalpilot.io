Slide 2 — Plan

Plan

Before You Click

◆ Define your entry price before the market opens
◆ Set your stop loss — know where you’re wrong
◆ Know your target — where you take profit
◆ Write your thesis — why this trade?
Signal Pilot
Slide 3 — Execute
2
Execute

Follow the Plan Exactly

◆ Enter at your planned level — not before, not after
◆ No improvising mid-trade — emotions lie
◆ Respect your stop — moving it is not executing
◆ Trust the process, not the feeling
Signal Pilot
Slide 4 — Review
3
Review

Learn From Every Trade

◆ Journal the outcome — win or loss, write it down
◆ Did you follow your plan? That matters more than P&L
◆ Learn from wins AND losses equally
◆ Adjust the system, not the next impulse
