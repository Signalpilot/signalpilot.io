BEFORE YOU CLICK

Plan the Trade

E
Entry Price
Define exactly where you'll enter. No guessing in the moment.
S
Stop Loss
Know where you're wrong before you're wrong.
T
Take Profit
Decide your target. Greed at entry prevents discipline at profit.
Y
Your Why
Write your thesis. Why this trade? What do you see?

Slide 3 — Execute
2
WHEN PRICE MOVES

Trade the Plan

P
Planned Level
Enter at your price. Not before. Not after. Discipline here separates winners.
D
Don't Improvise
Emotions make terrible traders. Your plan knows more than you do in the moment.
R
Respect Stops
Moving your stop is not \"managing\" — it's giving up. Exit at your level.
T
Trust Process
One trade doesn't prove anything. The process is proven across hundreds.

Slide 4 — Review
3
AFTER THE CLOSE

Review the Trade

J
Journal It
Win or loss — write it down. Your journal is your coach.
P
Process Over Profit
Did you follow your plan? That matters more than the P&L.
L
Learn Equally
Losses teach you more than wins. Study both with equal focus.
A
Adjust System
Change your system when data says so — not when emotions demand it.
