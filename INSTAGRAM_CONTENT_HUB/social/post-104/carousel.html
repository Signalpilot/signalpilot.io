Slide 2 — Five Traps

Five Psychological Traps

Every trader falls into these. The difference is how quickly you recognise and escape them.

F
FOMO
Chasing entries after a move has already happened — buying the top, selling the bottom
R
Revenge Trading
Doubling down after a loss to “win it back” — emotion replaces logic
O
Overconfidence
A winning streak makes you feel invincible — you increase size and abandon rules
A
Analysis Paralysis
Too many indicators, too many timeframes — you never actually pull the trigger
L
Loss Aversion
Holding losers too long and cutting winners too early — the opposite of what works

Slide 4 — Frameworks

Four Mental Frameworks

Rewire your thinking with these core principles. Every profitable trader operates from this foundation.

P
Process Over Outcome
A losing trade taken with discipline is better than a winning trade taken on impulse
T
Think in Probabilities
No single trade matters — focus on your edge over 100 trades, not the next one
D
Detach From Money
Think in R-multiples, not dollars — remove the emotional weight from each trade
U
Accept Uncertainty
You cannot control the market — you can only control your risk and your behaviour
