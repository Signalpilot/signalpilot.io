Slide 5 — How to Use MAs

How to Use MAs Correctly

Context tool, not an entry signal

✓
Do Use for trend context — are we above or below key averages?
✓
Do Combine with structure levels — support, resistance, and price action
✓
Do Treat as confirmation, not entry — validate what you already see
✕
Don't Use as standalone buy/sell signals — crossovers alone are not enough
✕
Don't Expect early entries from lagging indicators — that's not what they do
