THE FORMULA
Calculate Your Position Size
POSITION SIZE FORMULA
Position Size =
( Account x Risk % ) / Stop Distance
Risk the same dollar amount on every trade.
Not the same number of shares. Not the same percentage of price.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Account Size $10,000
Risk Per Trade 2% = $200
Entry Price $50.00
Stop Loss $45.00
Stop Distance $5.00
Position Size $200 / $5 = 40 shares

THE TRUTH
What Position Sizing Really Teaches
Survival First
You cannot compound what you have lost. The first rule of trading is to protect the capital that lets you trade tomorrow.
Math Over Emotion
Sizing is a formula, not a feeling. When you know your max loss before entry, fear loses its power over your decisions.
Consistency Wins
Same risk, every trade. No doubling down on conviction. No going light on uncertainty. Equal risk creates equal data.
2% Is Not Conservative
It is strategic. Professional traders know that the edge plays out over hundreds of trades. Survival is the strategy.

"The goal is not to win big on one trade. The goal is to stay in the game long enough for your edge to compound."
