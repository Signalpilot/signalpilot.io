What Changes Live

Real money triggers psychological shifts paper trading never prepared you for.

⚠
Real Money = Real Fear Losses hurt differently when capital is at stake. Your amygdala overrides your strategy.

⚠
FOMO Intensifies Every missed trade feels like lost money. You start chasing entries you wouldn't take on paper.
Emotional impact: 88%
⚠
Revenge Trading Temptation The urge to win it back immediately. One loss becomes three when discipline breaks.
Emotional impact: 92%
⚠
Execution Pressure Hesitation on entries, early exits on winners, moved stops on losers. Precision degrades.
