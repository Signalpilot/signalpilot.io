The Framework

Phase Awareness Is
the Real Edge

Knowing the phase changes everything. It does not tell you what to trade. It tells you how to trade.

  • 1
    Identify the Phase First
    Before any entry, ask: where are we in the cycle? TD, IGN, WRN, CAP, or BDN? This single question eliminates half of all bad trades.
  • 2
    Match Strategy to Phase
    Breakout strategies work in IGN. Mean-reversion works in WRN. Accumulation works in TD. Never force a strategy into the wrong context.
  • 3
    Watch for Transitions
    The edge lives at the phase boundary. When IGN becomes WRN, when WRN becomes CAP—that is where fortunes are made or destroyed.
  • 4
    Let Pentarch Confirm
    Emotion says "this time is different." The Pentarch indicator reads what the cycle actually says. Trust the data over the narrative.
