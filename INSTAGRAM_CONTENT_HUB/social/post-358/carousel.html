Core Rules

The 5 Pillars

1
Be Respectful — Treat every member with dignity. No personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.
2
No Financial Advice — Share ideas, not advice. Always disclaim that you are not a financial advisor.
3
Stay On Topic — Use the correct channels for your discussion. Off-topic goes in #general-chat.
4
No Spam or Self-Promo — Do not advertise services, products, or other servers without approval.
5
Protect Privacy — Never share personal info, DMs, or screenshots without consent.

Getting Help

Stuck? Lost? Have a question? Here is exactly how to get the help you need.

1
Search first — Use Discord search and check pinned messages. Your question may already be answered.
2
Choose the right channel — Platform issues go to #bug-reports. Strategy questions go to #education.
3
Be specific — Include screenshots, error messages, and steps to reproduce the problem.
4
Be patient — Mods and community members are volunteers. Give it time before re-posting.
5
Tag @Moderator — For urgent issues or rule violations, tag the mod team directly.

Enforcement

Reporting & Tips

🚨 How to Report
Use Discord's built-in report feature (right-click the message) or DM a moderator directly. Include evidence: screenshots, timestamps, and context.
🔒
Reports are confidential — Your identity is never shared with the reported user.
⚖️
Fair process — Mods review every report. Warnings come before bans, except for zero-tolerance violations.
🔄
Appeals exist — If you believe a decision was wrong, reach out to a senior mod with your case.
🌟
Be a role model — The best communities are shaped by members who lead by example every day.
