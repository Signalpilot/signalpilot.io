Slide 4 — What To Do Instead
04 / 05

What To Do Instead

The fix isn’t to double down. It’s to respect your original thesis and protect your capital.

C
Cut at Your Original Stop
Your stop was set for a reason. Honor it. The market gives infinite chances — your account doesn’t.
EFFECTIVENESS — 92%
R
Re-Evaluate, Don’t Rescue
Close the trade. Reassess with fresh eyes. If the thesis still holds, re-enter with proper risk.
EFFECTIVENESS — 88%
S
Size for the Worst Case
Position size so that hitting your stop is uncomfortable, not catastrophic.
EFFECTIVENESS — 85%

Cut once. Reassess. Re-enter clean. That’s discipline.
