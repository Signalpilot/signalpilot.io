THE CONCEPT
All Positions Closed
Before the Bell
D
DAY TRADING
Opening and closing all trades within the same session. No overnight holds. Uses 1-minute to 15-minute charts. Requires full attention during market hours.
T
TIMEFRAMES
5-minute and 15-minute for entries. 1-hour and 4-hour for context and bias. Trades last minutes to hours. Fast decisions, fast feedback.
!
REQUIREMENTS
Full attention during market hours. Fast decisions under pressure. Strict discipline, reliable internet, adequate capital, and a tested strategy.

Slide 3 — Pros vs Cons
03 / 08
THE TRADEOFFS
More Opportunities.
More Pressure.
ADVANTAGES
  • No overnight or weekend risk
  • Faster feedback loop
  • More trade opportunities
  • Daily fresh start
  • Income potential each session
CHALLENGES
  • High stress, high intensity
  • Time consuming (full sessions)
  • Higher commission costs
  • Noise-heavy lower timeframes
  • Pattern day trader rules (PDT)
REALITY CHECK
Most day traders lose money in the first 2 years. The fast feedback loop that makes day trading attractive also makes bad habits form faster. Proper education is non-negotiable.

Slide 4 — Daily Routine
04 / 08
THE ROUTINE
A Day Trader's
5-Step Session
1
Pre-Market Prep
Review overnight price action, mark key levels, check the economic calendar, and define your bias for the day. Never trade without a plan.
2
Opening Range (First 30 Min)
Observe the opening range. Don't rush in. Let the market reveal its hand. The first 30 minutes are for watching, not trading.
3
Execute Your Setups
When your pre-defined setups appear at your pre-marked levels, execute without hesitation. The plan was made with a clear mind. Trust it.
4
Mid-Day Assessment
Around lunchtime, assess your P&L and emotional state. If you're up, protect gains. If you're down, don't revenge trade. Consider stopping early.
5
Close and Review
Flatten all positions before close. Journal every trade: what worked, what didn't, what you'll improve tomorrow. The journal is the edge.

CRITICAL SKILLS
The 6 Skills
Every Day Trader Needs
R
Reading Price Action
Understanding what each candle means in context. No indicators can replace the ability to read raw price.
FOUNDATION
L
Level Identification
Day trading is a levels game. Support, resistance, VWAP, previous day's high/low. Trades happen at levels.
ESSENTIAL
D
Discipline
Sticking to the plan when adrenaline says otherwise. The best day traders are the most boring ones.
NON-NEGOTIABLE
S
Speed of Execution
Entries and exits must be precise. Pre-set your orders. Use hotkeys. Milliseconds matter when the market moves fast.
MECHANICAL
E
Emotional Control
Two losses in a row and emotions spike. The third trade must follow the system, not your feelings.
PSYCHOLOGY
J
Journaling
Record every trade, every emotion, every deviation. Your journal reveals patterns your memory never will.

Slide 6 — Common Traps

COMMON TRAPS
Three Mistakes That
Destroy New Day Traders
1
OVERTRADING
Taking 20+ trades a day because you're "in the zone." More trades ≠ more profit. Quality over quantity. 3–5 well-planned trades is enough.
2
REVENGE TRADING
After a loss, doubling size to "get it back." This is how one bad trade becomes five. Walk away. The market will be there tomorrow.
3
SKIPPING THE JOURNAL
If you don't review your trades, you cannot improve. The journal is where patterns emerge, mistakes get caught, and your edge gets refined.

Slide 7 — Day vs Swing
07 / 08
?
DAY VS SWING
Which Style Fits
Your Life?
Time Available
Day trading needs 4–6 hours of focused screen time daily. Swing needs 30 minutes. Be honest about your schedule.
Personality Type
Day traders thrive on intensity and fast decisions. Swing traders prefer patience and analysis. Neither is better — just different.
Capital Requirements
US PDT rules require $25K minimum for day trading. Swing trading has no such restriction. Start where you can.
Learning Curve
Day trading has a steeper curve because mistakes happen faster. Swing trading gives you more time to think and learn.

"The best trading style is the one you can execute consistently without burning out."
