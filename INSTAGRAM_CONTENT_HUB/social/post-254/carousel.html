Slide 3 — Amateur Mindset
03 / 08
THE AMATEUR TRAP

“How Much
Can I Make?”

01
Position size based on greed
“I’ll go big on this one because the setup looks perfect.” Famous last words. You size up when you feel confident, not when the math supports it.
02
Stop loss as an afterthought
Entry first, stop later. Or worse — no stop at all. “I’ll just watch it.” You will not just watch it. You’ll panic at the worst moment.
03
Focuses on the win rate
Celebrates 8 wins in a row. Ignores that the next 2 losses wiped all of them out. Win rate means nothing without risk-per-trade math.
THE COST
One bad trade can erase 47 good ones. This isn’t a metaphor — it’s simple math when you risk 10% on a “sure thing” and 0.5% on everything else.

Slide 4 — Professional Mindset
04 / 08
THE PROFESSIONAL EDGE

“How Much
Can I Lose?”

01
Define max loss before entry
Before anything else: “Where am I wrong?” The stop comes first. The entry is built around the stop, not the other way around.
02
Size position from stop distance
Stop distance determines position size. Not conviction. Not excitement. If the stop is wide, the position is small. The math is non-negotiable.
03
Accept the loss before it happens
Before clicking buy: “I am risking $100 on this trade. If I lose it, that is fine.” If you can’t say that sincerely, your position is too big.
THE EDGE
When you control the downside, the upside takes care of itself. You stop chasing. You stop revenge trading. You trade with clarity because the worst case is already priced in.

Slide 7 — Pre-Trade Ritual
07 / 08
YOUR RITUAL

Before Every
Single Trade

STEP 01
Where Am I Wrong?
Identify the invalidation level. Not where you hope it won’t go — where the trade thesis breaks. That’s your stop.
STEP 02
Calculate Stop Distance
Measure the gap between entry and stop. This is your risk per unit. The wider the stop, the smaller the position.
Risk per unit = Entry − Stop
STEP 03
Size from the Stop
Divide your max dollar risk by the stop distance. The result is your position size. No rounding up. No exceptions.
Size = (Account × 1%) ÷ Stop distance
STEP 04
Accept It
Say out loud: “I am willing to lose this amount.” If you hesitate, cut the size in half. The position should feel boring, not exciting.
  • ✓ Let the math decide. Not your conviction.
  • ✓ The upside handles itself.
