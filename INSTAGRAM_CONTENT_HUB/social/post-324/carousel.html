THE HABITS

5 Rules for Asymmetry

1
Never Risk More Than 1%
Per trade. Non-negotiable. This ensures no single loss can damage your account or your psychology.
2
Minimum 2:1 R:R
Don't take a trade unless the target is at least 2x your stop. This is the floor, not the ceiling.
3
Let Winners Run
Use partials: take 50% at 2:1, move stop to breakeven, let the rest ride. This is how 3:1 becomes 5:1.
4
Cut Losers at the Stop
No "giving it room." No "it'll come back." Hit the stop, close the trade. Next setup. Mechanical.
5
Track Your Avg Win/Loss Ratio
Pull up your trade history monthly. Average win size divided by average loss size. Below 2:1? Fix it.

The Paradox
You can be wrong on 6 out of 10 trades and still be highly profitable. The trick is making your winners 3x bigger than your losers. That's asymmetry. That's the edge. It only works if you accept that losing is part of the process.
Tonight's Homework
Calculate Your Asymmetry Score
Pull up your last 20 trades. Calculate: average win size / average loss size. If it's below 2:1, your next priority is letting winners run longer and cutting losers faster. This single metric predicts profitability better than win rate.
