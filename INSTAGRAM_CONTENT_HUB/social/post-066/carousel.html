Slide 7 — Common Mistakes
07 / 09

Fibonacci Mistakes

The 4 errors that turn a good tool into a liability.

1
Over-Drawing Levels
Too many Fib lines on one chart = too much noise. Pick 1-2 key swings, not every minor wick.
2
Trading Without Context
A Fib level at 61.8% means nothing in isolation. Check trend, support/resistance, and volume first.
3
Ignoring Timeframes
A 4H Fib level might fail if a 1D trend is stronger. Always respect the higher timeframe context.
4
No Risk Management
A Fib bounce point is NOT a guaranteed reversal. Still risk a fixed % of your account.
