Reading the Chart

Four Momentum Signals

01
Candle Size
Growing bodies = increasing momentum. Shrinking bodies = momentum is fading. The body tells the story — not the wicks.
02
Candle Overlap
Little overlap between candles = strong conviction. Heavy overlap = indecision. Buyers and sellers are fighting.
03
Volume Confirm
Rising momentum + rising volume = genuine move. Rising momentum + declining volume = be cautious.
04
Oscillator Fade
Momentum dies 2–3 bars before price reverses. The oscillator warns you before the chart does. Watch it.
"Momentum fades before price reverses.
Watch the oscillator."

The Framework

Momentum + Volume Matrix

Genuine
Strong + Rising Vol
The move has conviction. Participants are entering. Trend likely continues. Highest-confidence setup.
Warning
Strong + Falling Vol
Price moves fast but participants are leaving. The move is losing fuel. Be cautious — exhaustion may follow.
Fading
Weak + Rising Vol
Volume is entering but price can't move. Absorption phase. Big players may be accumulating or distributing.
Trap
Weak + Falling Vol
No conviction, no participants. Indecision zone. Do not enter. Wait for momentum to return before acting.
Common Mistake
Chasing a move with strong momentum. By the time momentum is obvious to everyone, the easy part is over. The best entries come when momentum is about to shift — not after it already has.

The Truth
P

Momentum Is the Pilot's Edge

01
Speed Reveals Intent
Price direction tells you where. Momentum tells you why — and whether it will continue.
02
Fading Before Reversing
Momentum dies 2–3 bars before price turns. Read the fade, not the reversal.
03
Volume Must Confirm
Strong momentum with declining volume is a lie. Always check both — they must tell the same story.
04
Trade With, Not Against
Enter with momentum, exit when it fades. Never fight the speed of the market.
