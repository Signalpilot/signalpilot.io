Slide 6 — Where to Find Mentors
06 / 07

Where to Find
Mentors

The right mentor is not always the loudest voice. Look for consistent results, genuine transparency, and a teaching style that matches how you learn.

🌐
Trading Communities
Active Discord servers, Slack groups, and forums where experienced traders share analysis daily. Look for communities with verified track records and moderated discussions.
🎓
Educational Platforms
Structured mentorship programs with curriculum, live sessions, and personal feedback. The best platforms pair you with a mentor who trades the same style you want to learn.
📈
Successful Traders You Respect
Reach out directly to traders whose approach resonates with you. Many experienced traders are open to mentoring if you show dedication, ask good questions, and respect their time.
Red Flags to Watch

Avoid anyone who guarantees profits, refuses to show verified results, or charges thousands upfront without a trial period. Real mentors are transparent about losses, focus on process over P&L screenshots, and prioritize your growth over their revenue.
