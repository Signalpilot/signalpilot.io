Slide 2 — Real Breakout

Signs It’s Real

A genuine breakout leaves evidence. Look for these four confirmation signals.

V
Volume Spike
CONVICTION SIGNAL
Real breakouts come with a surge in volume. If volume is flat or declining, institutions aren’t behind the move.
C
Clean Close Beyond
PRICE CONFIRMATION
The candle must close beyond the level — not just wick through. Wicks are noise; closes are commitment.
F
Follow-Through
MOMENTUM PROOF
The next candle continues the move. Follow-through proves that demand (or supply) is sustaining the breakout.
S
Structure Support
CONTEXT ALIGNMENT
The breakout aligns with the higher-timeframe trend or market structure. Context confirms direction.

Slide 3 — Fakeout Signs

Signs It’s a Trap

Fakeouts are designed to look convincing. Watch for these warning signs before committing.

L
Low Volume Break
WEAK CONVICTION
A break on declining or low volume means retail is pushing — not institutions. It’s a trap waiting to snap back.
W
Wick-Only Close
FALSE SIGNAL
Price wicks beyond the level but closes back inside. The market tested and rejected — not broke through.
R
Quick Reversal
LIQUIDITY GRAB
Price breaks out then immediately reverses. This is a stop hunt — institutions grabbing liquidity before moving the opposite direction.
