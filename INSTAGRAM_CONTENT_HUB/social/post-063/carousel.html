COMPARISON

The Difference

BREAKOUT
✓
  • High volume
  • Clean close beyond
  • Momentum follows
  • Holds above level
FAKEOUT
✗
  • Low volume
  • Wick only
  • Reverses quickly
  • Falls back inside

Volume and follow-through separate the two. Every time.
