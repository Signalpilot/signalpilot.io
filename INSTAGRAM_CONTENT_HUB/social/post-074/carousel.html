THE FIX
Stop Being Early
W
STRATEGY 01
WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION
Don't enter on prediction. Wait for price to confirm your level with a reaction, wick rejection, or engulfing candle.
S
STRATEGY 02
USE WIDER STOPS
Place your stop beyond the liquidity sweep zone. Give the trade room to breathe without getting shaken out early.
G
STRATEGY 03
SCALE IN GRADUALLY
Don't go all-in at one price. Split your entry across 2-3 levels so one sweep doesn't take you out entirely.
