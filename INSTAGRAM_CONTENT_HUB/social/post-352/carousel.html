Markets are inherently uncertain. No indicator, system, or guru can predict the future with certainty. The sooner you accept this, the sooner you start protecting your capital.

Admitting uncertainty isn’t weakness — it’s the foundation of intelligent risk management.

The Trap

Overconfidence
vs. Humility

⚠ Overconfident

“I know exactly where this is going.”

Oversized positions, no stop-loss, revenge trades after losses.

★ Humble

“I have an edge, but I could be wrong.”

Proper sizing, defined risk, accepts outcomes gracefully.

⚠ Result

Account blowups

One bad trade can wipe months of gains.

★ Result

Consistent growth

Small, controlled losses compound into longevity.

Slide 5 — 5 Situations
05 / 08
When To Say It

5 Situations to Say
“I Don’t Know”

1
Before a News Event FOMC, NFP, CPI — nobody knows the number or the reaction. Step aside or reduce size.
2
Conflicting Signals When your indicators disagree, the honest answer is “I don’t know” — and that means no trade.
3
After a Winning Streak Feeling invincible? That is exactly when “I don’t know” protects you from oversizing.
4
On an Unfamiliar Asset New market, new rules. Admit you are learning before committing real capital.
5
When Someone Asks Your Bias “I don’t have one yet” is more valuable than guessing for clout.
