Slide 2 — Process

The 5-Step Backtest Process

1
Define Your Rules
Write exact entry, exit, stop loss, and take profit rules before touching a chart
2
Pick Your Sample
Minimum 100 trades across multiple market conditions — trending, ranging, volatile
3
Scroll Bar by Bar
Replay the chart and mark each setup. No peeking ahead — simulate real-time decisions
4
Record Everything
Log every trade in a spreadsheet: entry, exit, result, R:R, and any notes
5
Analyse the Data
Calculate win rate, average R:R, expectancy, and max drawdown from your results

Slide 3 — Comparison

Bad vs Good Backtesting

Bad Backtest
✗ Cherry-picking winning trades
✗ Testing only in trending markets
✗ Sample size of 10-20 trades
✗ Adjusting rules mid-test
Good Backtest
✓ Taking every valid signal
✓ All market conditions included
✓ 100+ trade sample minimum
✓ Rules locked before starting

Slide 4 — Metrics

Four Metrics That Matter

01
Win Rate
Percentage of trades that hit TP. Meaningless without R:R context — 35% can still be profitable.
02
Average R:R
Mean reward-to-risk across all trades. Higher R:R compensates for lower win rate.
03
Expectancy
(Win% × Avg Win) − (Loss% × Avg Loss). Must be positive or the strategy loses money.
04
Max Drawdown
Largest peak-to-trough decline. If you can't stomach this drawdown live, reduce your risk.
