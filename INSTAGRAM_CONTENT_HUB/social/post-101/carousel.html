Slide 2 — Process

The 5-Step Backtest Process

A rigorous, repeatable framework for validating any trading strategy before risking capital.

1
Define Your Rules
Write exact entry, exit, stop loss, and take profit rules before touching a chart
2
Pick Your Sample
Minimum 100 trades across multiple market conditions — trending, ranging, volatile
3
Scroll Bar by Bar
Replay the chart and mark each setup. No peeking ahead — simulate real-time decisions
4
Record Everything
Log every trade in a spreadsheet: entry, exit, result, R:R, and any notes
5
Analyse the Data
Calculate win rate, average R:R, expectancy, and max drawdown from your results

Slide 4 — Metrics

Four Metrics That Matter

These numbers separate profitable systems from losing ones. Track all four, not just win rate.

W
01
Win Rate
Percentage of trades that hit TP. Meaningless without R:R context — 35% can still be profitable.
R
02
Average R:R
Mean reward-to-risk across all trades. Higher R:R compensates for lower win rate.
E
03
Expectancy
(Win% × Avg Win) − (Loss% × Avg Loss). Must be positive or the strategy loses money.
D
04
Max Drawdown
Largest peak-to-trough decline. If you can’t stomach this drawdown live, reduce your risk.

Slide 5 — Truth
B

The Deeper Truth

Test Before You Fly
You wouldn’t fly a plane without testing it first — don’t trade a strategy untested.
Confidence Survives Drawdowns
Backtesting builds the conviction that holds you steady through losing streaks.
Forward-Test First
Validate with small size in live markets before committing full risk capital.
Re-Test Quarterly
Markets evolve and so should your data. Stale backtests create stale edges.

“The backtest doesn’t guarantee the future — it earns your right to trade the present.”
