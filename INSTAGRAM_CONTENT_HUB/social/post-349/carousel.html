Why Multi-Chart?

A single chart shows a single perspective. Multi-chart layouts let you see correlated pairs, higher timeframes, and different indicators simultaneously -- without ever switching tabs.

👁
Full Context
See the daily trend while trading the 4H entry. No more guessing the bigger picture.
⚡
Faster Decisions
Compare correlated assets side by side. Spot divergences and confirmations instantly.
📈
Indicator Layering
Separate volume analysis from price action. Each panel has a clear, focused purpose.
🛠
Custom Workflows
Build layouts tailored to your strategy. Scalpers, swing traders, and investors each need different views.
Professional traders never trade from a single chart. They build cockpits that show the entire market at a glance.

Slide 4 — Recommended Setup

Recommended Setup

3
Three-Panel Layout
Recommended
Main chart: Your primary trading timeframe (e.g., 4H)
Higher TF: One step up for trend context (e.g., Daily)
Correlated pair: Related asset for confirmation (e.g., DXY vs EUR/USD)
1
Set Your Main Chart
Open your primary asset and timeframe in the largest panel. This is where you execute trades and mark entries.
2
Add Higher Timeframe
Use the second panel for the same asset on a higher timeframe. If your main is 4H, use the Daily here for trend direction.
3
Add Correlated Asset
Place a related instrument in the third panel. For BTC trades, add ETH or the total crypto market cap to confirm sector movement.
4
Save as Template
Save the entire layout including indicators and symbols. Load it with one click at the start of each trading session.

Syncing Features

TradingView lets you synchronize chart behaviors across panels. These features turn separate charts into a unified analysis cockpit.

✚
Crosshair Sync
Move your cursor on one chart and the crosshair follows on all panels. See the same moment in time across every timeframe.
Linked
🕑
Time Sync
Scroll through time on one chart and all panels follow. Navigate history without losing your place on other charts.
Synced
📄
Symbol Sync
Change the symbol on one chart and all linked panels update. Quickly scan through your watchlist across multiple timeframes.
Group A
🔧
Drawing Sync
Draw a trendline on the daily and it appears on the 4H. Keep key levels visible across every timeframe without redrawing.
Shared
Enable crosshair sync first. It is the single feature that transforms separate charts into a connected analysis workspace.

Slide 7 — Pro Tips + Mistakes
07 / 08

Pro Tips + Mistakes

⚡
Save Multiple Layout Templates
Create separate layouts for different strategies. A scalping layout (1m + 5m + 15m) is very different from a swing layout (4H + Daily + Weekly). Switch between them in one click.
📚
Keep Your Main Panel Largest
The chart you trade from should always occupy the most screen space. Confirmation panels can be smaller -- they are for context, not execution.
⚠️
Do Not Overload Panels
Placing five indicators on every chart defeats the purpose. Each panel should have a clear role with one or two focused indicators. Clutter creates confusion, not clarity.
🚫
Avoid Redundant Timeframes
Displaying 1H and 2H side by side adds almost no new information. Space your timeframes at least 3-4x apart: 1H and 4H, or 4H and Daily.
📝 Homework
Open a 3-panel layout in TradingView
Assign Pentarch to Daily, Janus Atlas to 4H, Volume Oracle to 1H
Enable crosshair sync across all three panels
Save the layout as a template for your primary asset
