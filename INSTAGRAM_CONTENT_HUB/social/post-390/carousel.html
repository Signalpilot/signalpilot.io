Slide 4 — Linked Features

Linked Features

Synchronise your charts so they behave as one unified workspace. Link crosshairs, symbols, intervals, and drawings across every pane.

Crosshair Sync
Hover on one chart, see the crosshair on all. Perfect for comparing price action at the same point in time.
Linked
Symbol Sync
Change one chart’s symbol and the rest follow. Quickly scan through your watchlist on all timeframes at once.
Linked
Interval Sync
Switch timeframes on one chart and all linked charts update together. Maintain your multi-timeframe ratios.
Linked
Drawing Sync
Draw a trendline or zone on one chart and see it reflected across all linked views. One annotation, every perspective.
Linked
