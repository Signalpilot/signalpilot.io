Slide 2 — The Formula

The Position Sizing Formula

Core Formula
Position Size = Risk $ ÷ Distance to Stop
Example — $10,000 Account
Account Balance $10,000
Risk Per Trade (1%) $100
Stop Loss Distance 50 pips
Position Size $100 ÷ 50 = 2 units/pip

Slide 3 — Comparison

Fixed Lot vs Risk-Based Sizing

Fixed Lot
✗ Same size regardless of volatility
✗ Wide stops = massive risk
✗ Tight stops = tiny reward
✗ Inconsistent equity curve
Risk-Based
✓ Adapts to each trade's volatility
✓ Same $ risk every time
✓ Normalised P&L across setups
✓ Smooth, compounding growth

Slide 4 — Four Rules

Four Rules of Sizing

01
Risk 1-2% Max
Never risk more than 2% of your account on a single trade. 1% is ideal.
02
Size After Stop
Set your stop loss first, then calculate your position size from the distance.
03
Scale Down on Streaks
After 3 consecutive losses, cut size to 0.5% until confidence returns.
04
Account for Correlation
Correlated positions stack risk. Treat multiple entries in one direction as one trade.
