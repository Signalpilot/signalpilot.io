Slide 2 — Formula
Formula
The Position Sizing Formula
Core Formula
Position Size = Risk $ ÷ Distance to Stop
Example — $10,000 Account
Account Balance $10,000
Risk Per Trade (1%) $100
Stop Loss Distance 50 pips
Position Size $100 ÷ 50 = 2 units/pip

Slide 4 — Rules
Rules
Four Rules of Sizing
01
Risk 1-2% Max
Never risk more than 2% of your account on a single trade. 1% is ideal for longevity.
02
Size After Stop
Set your stop loss first, then calculate position size from the distance — not the other way around.
03
Scale Down on Streaks
After 3 consecutive losses, cut size to 0.5% until confidence and edge return to baseline.
04
Account for Correlation
Correlated positions stack risk. Treat multiple entries in one direction as a single trade exposure.
