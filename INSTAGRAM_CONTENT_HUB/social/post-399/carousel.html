Treat Everyone with Kindness
Assume good intent. Offer grace before judgement.
Core

Rule

Value

We are all learning. Every expert was once a beginner.

Slide 3 — No Financial Advice
⚠

No Financial Advice

This community exists to educate, not to recommend. Share your analysis and thinking — never tell others what to buy or sell.

Say This
"This is what I'm watching"
"Here's my analysis on..."
"I'm studying this setup"
Not This
"You should buy this"
"Sell now, trust me"
"Guaranteed 10x play"
Why This Matters
Financial advice without qualification is dangerous. We protect our community by sharing education, not recommendations. This keeps everyone safe — including you.
Principle
Protect yourself and others. Education over instruction.

Slide 4 — Share to Educate

Share to Educate

The best way to learn is to teach. When you share your thinking process, you solidify your own understanding — and help others grow.

I
Show Your Thinking Process
Walk through your reasoning, not just the conclusion. Show the "why" behind the "what."
II
Explain Your Analysis
Mark up your charts. Label your levels. Give context to your observations.
III
This Isn't a Flex Zone
Nobody learns from a screenshot of P&L. Teach the process, not the profit.
IV
Wins and Losses Both Teach
A well-analysed loss is worth more than an unexplained win. Share both with humility.
The goal is not to look smart.
The goal is to make everyone smarter.
