Your Rules

Three Limits. No Exceptions.

📉
Per Trade
1-2%
Maximum risk on any single position. No trade is worth more.
📊
Per Day
3-5%
Daily loss limit. Hit it? Walk away. The market will be there tomorrow.
📈
Per Week
5-10%
Weekly drawdown cap. Protects you from compounding bad streaks.
Why These Numbers?
These limits protect you from the version of yourself that can’t think straight. After consecutive losses, judgment deteriorates. The rules protect your capital when your mind can’t.

The Paradox

Protection Creates Profit

The Capital Preservation Paradox
Traders who focus on protecting capital often make more than those chasing gains. This seems counterintuitive. It’s not.
1
They Stay in the Game Longer
Blowups end careers. Small losses are survivable. Survival is the prerequisite of success.
2
They Compound Without Blowups
Steady growth beats spectacular gains followed by spectacular losses. Math favors consistency.
3
They Trade With Clarity, Not Fear
When your downside is defined, fear dissolves. You make decisions from logic, not emotion.
