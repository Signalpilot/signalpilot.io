WHAT THIS MEANS
The Oath Decoded
"I will not chase."
Miss an entry, let it go. The cycle repeats. Chasing is how accounts bleed to death.
"I will not guess."
If signals conflict, stand aside. Guessing is gambling with extra steps.
"I will not hope."
Hope is not a strategy. Exit criteria either hit or they don't. Hope changes nothing.
"I will read the cycle."
Know where you are in the Pentarch cycle. Exhaustion? Ignition? The phase determines everything.
"I will follow the institutions."
Volume Oracle shows where money is positioned. Trade with the flow, not against it.
"I will wait for consensus."
The Arbiter speaks last. Four stars means strike. Anything less means patience.
