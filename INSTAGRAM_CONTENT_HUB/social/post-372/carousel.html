Common Risk
Guidelines

Professional traders define risk as a percentage of total account equity per trade. Here are the widely accepted tiers.

Conservative
0.5–1%
Lower drawdowns. Ideal for new traders or large accounts seeking capital preservation.
Moderate
1–2%
The sweet spot. Most professionals stay here. Balanced growth with manageable drawdowns.
Aggressive
2–3%
Higher reward potential but deeper drawdowns. Only for experienced traders with proven edge.

“Most professional traders risk between 1% and 2% of their account per trade. Simple. Non-negotiable.”

Slide 5 — Key Principles

Key Principles of
Position Sizing

Risk management is not about avoiding losses. It is about ensuring that no single loss or streak of losses can take you out of the game.

1
Risk Should Feel Uncomfortable, Not Devastating
If a loss would make you change your strategy or panic, you are risking too much. The right size lets you take the loss and move on without emotion.
2
Survive the Worst Losing Streak Imaginable
Plan for 20 losses in a row. If your risk percentage can survive that scenario, your position sizing is built to last. If not, reduce immediately.
3
Consistency Over Time Wins
Small, consistent risk produces compounding returns. Erratic sizing creates erratic results. The same percentage on every trade builds an edge over hundreds of trades.
4
Never Let a Winner Turn Into an Oversized Bet
A winning streak does not justify increasing risk. Stick to the percentage. Your account balance will naturally increase your dollar risk as it grows.

“The goal is not to make money on any single trade. The goal is to be around long enough for your edge to play out.”

When to
Adjust Risk

Your base risk should be fixed, but smart traders know when slight adjustments make sense. The key word is slight.

When
High confidence A+ setup with multiple confluences
→
Slight Increase
Move from 1% to 1.5%. Never double your standard risk.
When
Lower confidence setup or unclear market conditions
→
Reduce Risk
Drop to 0.5% or skip the trade entirely. Preservation first.
When
Volatile market or major news event approaching
→
Reduce Risk
Cut to 0.5% or widen stops. Volatility amplifies outcomes in both directions.
When
Account is growing steadily over time
→
Keep % Same
The percentage stays the same. Your dollar risk automatically scales with equity.
Homework

Open your trading journal and review your last 10 trades. For each one, calculate the actual percentage of account risked. Were you consistent? Were there trades where you exceeded your target risk? Build a position sizing checklist to run before every entry.
