THE CONCEPT
The Middle Path

S
SWING TRADING
Holding trades for days to weeks to capture larger price moves. Uses 4H and Daily charts. Fewer trades, bigger targets, less screen time.
T
TIMEFRAMES
Daily chart for direction. 4H chart for entry. Trades typically last 2–14 days. You check charts twice a day, not twice a minute.
W
WHO IT'S FOR
Traders with day jobs, lives, and patience. You don't need to watch every tick. Set your levels, place your orders, live your life.

Slide 4 — Ideal Setup
04 / 08
THE SETUP
Anatomy of an
Ideal Swing Trade
1
Identify the Trend
Daily chart shows a clear uptrend. Price above the 50-day MA. Higher highs and higher lows confirmed.
2
Wait for a Pullback
Price retraces to a key support level — the 50-day MA, a prior resistance-turned-support, or a Fibonacci level.
3
Confirm the Bounce
A bullish engulfing candle, hammer, or strong close above the level. Volume should increase on the bounce — this confirms buyers are stepping in.
4
Enter and Set Stop
Enter the next day. Stop below the MA or swing low. Target the prior swing high. Typical hold time: 5–10 days.
5
Manage the Trade
Trail your stop as the trade moves in your favour. Take partial profits at the first target. Let runners ride with a trailing stop.

Slide 5 — Key Factors

KEY FACTORS
The 6 Elements
of Successful Swings
T
Trend Alignment
Only swing trade in the direction of the higher timeframe trend. Fighting the trend kills swings.
CRITICAL
L
Level Quality
Enter at confluent levels where multiple factors stack. Random entries mean random results.
ESSENTIAL
R
Risk:Reward
Minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk. Swing setups naturally offer bigger targets. If R:R is below 2:1, skip it.
NON-NEGOTIABLE
V
Volume Confirmation
Volume should expand on the move you're trading and contract on pullbacks. Volume validates the swing.
CONFIRMATION
C
Calendar Awareness
Major events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) can gap your position overnight. Plan around the economic calendar.
RISK MGMT
P
Patience
The best swing setups take days to develop. Forcing entries out of boredom is the number one swing trading mistake.

Slide 6 — When to Avoid

WHEN NOT TO SWING
Three Environments That
Kill Swing Trades
1
CHOPPY / SIDEWAYS MARKETS
No trend means your swings get stopped out repeatedly. Check the higher timeframe first. No trend = no swing trades.
2
MAJOR NEWS EVENTS
FOMC, NFP, CPI releases can gap against you overnight. Either close before the event or widen stops and reduce size.
3
EARNINGS SEASON
Individual stocks can gap 10–20% on earnings. Never hold a swing through an earnings report unless you've sized for the gap risk.

THE EDGE
Why Swing Trading
Outperforms for Most People
Signal Quality
Daily chart signals have far less noise than 5-minute charts. Higher timeframe = higher probability setups with cleaner structure.
Compounding Effect
Fewer trades means lower commissions and less slippage. The savings compound dramatically over hundreds of trades per year.
Emotional Control
You're not reacting to every tick. Decisions happen with time and clarity, not in the heat of a 1-minute candle.
Life Balance
Check charts at open and close. That's it. No staring at screens all day. Trading should enhance your life, not consume it.
