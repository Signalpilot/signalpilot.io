THE SETUP
Anatomy of an
Ideal Swing Trade
1
Identify the Trend
Daily chart shows a clear trend. Price above the 50-day MA. Higher highs and higher lows confirmed. Don't fight the direction of the market.
2
Wait for a Pullback
Price retraces to a key support level — the 50-day MA, prior resistance-turned-support, or a Fibonacci retracement level. Patience is the setup.
3
Confirm the Bounce
A bullish engulfing candle, hammer, or strong close above the level. Volume should increase on the bounce — buyers are stepping in with conviction.
4
Enter and Set Stop
Enter on the next day's open. Stop below the moving average or swing low. Target the prior swing high. Typical hold time: 5–10 days.
5
Manage the Trade
Trail your stop as the trade moves in your favor. Take partial profits at the first target. Let the runner ride with a trailing stop behind swing structure.

KEY FACTORS
The 6 Elements
of Successful Swings
T
Trend Alignment
Only swing trade in the direction of the higher timeframe trend. Fighting the trend kills swings before they start.
CRITICAL
L
Level Quality
Enter at confluent levels where multiple factors stack — MA, fib, structure. Random entries produce random results.
ESSENTIAL
R
Risk:Reward
Minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk. Swing setups naturally offer bigger targets. If R:R is below 2:1, skip the trade entirely.
NON-NEGOTIABLE
V
Volume Confirm
Volume should expand on the move you're trading and contract on pullbacks. Volume validates the swing’s conviction.
CONFIRMATION
C
Calendar Aware
FOMC, NFP, CPI releases can gap your position overnight. Plan around the economic calendar or reduce size.
RISK MGMT
P
Patience
The best swing setups take days to develop. Forcing entries out of boredom is the #1 swing trading mistake.

WHEN NOT TO SWING
Three Environments That
Kill Swing Trades
1
CHOPPY / SIDEWAYS MARKETS
No trend means your swings get stopped out repeatedly. The market chops back and forth, eating your stops. Check the higher timeframe first. No trend = no swing trades.
2
MAJOR NEWS EVENTS
FOMC, NFP, CPI releases can gap against you overnight. A 2% gap wipes out your stop placement entirely. Either close before the event or widen stops and reduce size significantly.
3
EARNINGS SEASON
Individual stocks can gap 10–20% on earnings surprises. Never hold a swing through an earnings report unless you've specifically sized your position for maximum gap risk.
REMEMBER
The best swing traders know when not to trade. Sitting out bad conditions is not passive — it's the most profitable decision you can make. Preservation of capital enables future opportunity.
