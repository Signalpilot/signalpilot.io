Slide 2 — The Concept

THE CONCEPT
Every Bar Has
Two Dimensions
S
SPREAD (THE CANDLE)
The range from high to low. Wide spread = large price movement. Narrow spread = small movement. This is the headline — what happened.
V
VOLUME (THE TRUTH)
How many participants were involved. High volume = conviction. Low volume = doubt. This is the story — whether the headline is real.
=
VSA = SPREAD + VOLUME
By analyzing the relationship between spread and volume, you see what the big players are really doing — not just what price shows.

VSA MATRIX
The 4 Combinations
That Matter
WIDE + HIGH VOL
Strong Conviction

Slide 4 — The Framework
04 / 08
THE FRAMEWORK
Reading VSA
Step by Step
1
Identify the Key Level
Is price at support, resistance, or a significant structure level? VSA signals mean different things at different locations.
2
Measure the Spread
Compare the current bar's range to the prior 20 bars. Is it wide, narrow, or average? Relative comparison matters more than absolutes.
3
Compare the Volume
Is volume above or below average? A "high volume" bar only matters relative to what came before. Always compare — never judge in isolation.
4
Read the Combination
Match the spread + volume combination to the four patterns. This tells you whether the move is genuine, weak, absorptive, or meaningless.
5
Watch the Follow-Through
Does the next bar confirm? Absorption at support followed by an up bar = confirmation. No follow-through = the signal was noise.
