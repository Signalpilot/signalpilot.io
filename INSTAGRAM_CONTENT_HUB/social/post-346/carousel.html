Slide 3 — The Pattern

The Same Traps

Trap I
Chasing the Move
Entering after the move already happened. Buying tops, selling bottoms. Reacting instead of positioning.
Trap II
Ignoring Volume
Price without volume is a lie. Most traders never learn to read the conviction behind the candle.
Trap III
Fighting the Cycle
Markets breathe in phases. Trading against the current phase is like swimming against the tide.
Trap IV
Emotional Execution
Fear and greed replace logic. The plan dissolves the moment the chart moves against you.
The Realisation
The traps are not random. They are structural. They repeat because human psychology repeats. And if they repeat — they can be mapped.
