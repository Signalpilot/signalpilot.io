Slide 3 — Anatomy

Anatomy of a Sweep

1
Liquidity Pools Form
Equal highs/lows and obvious S/R — stops cluster at predictable levels
2
Engineered Move
Price is driven into the pool with a sharp, aggressive candle
3
Absorption
Institutions absorb triggered orders, filling positions at optimal prices
4
Displacement
The real move begins — price displaces in the opposite direction
5
Confirmation
A CHoCH or BOS confirms the reversal and validates the entry

Slide 4 — Trading Tips

How to Trade Sweeps

W
Wait for Completion
Never anticipate the reversal before the wick closes
D
Look for Displacement
A strong candle in the opposite direction confirms intent
O
Combine with Order Blocks
Sweeps into OBs create the highest-probability entries
H
Check HTF Bias
Trade sweeps that align with the higher timeframe trend
S
Stop Beyond the Sweep
Your stop goes past the liquidity grab, not at it
