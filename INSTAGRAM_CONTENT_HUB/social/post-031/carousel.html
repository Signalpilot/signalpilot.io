The Truth

It's a Neuroscience Problem

When you lose money, your brain treats it as a threat to survival.

The same circuits that kept our ancestors alive now make us revenge trade.

03 / 08
After a Loss — Step 1
Amygdala Activates
Your brain's threat detection center lights up. It doesn't distinguish between "lost money" and "tiger in the bushes."

Both trigger fight or flight.
SIGNALPILOT
04 / 08
After a Loss — Step 2
Cortisol Floods Your System
Stress hormone surges. Your prefrontal cortex — the rational part — goes offline.

You're now making decisions with your survival brain.

05 / 08
The Cycle

The Revenge Trade Loop

  1. You NEED to make it back
  2. You take a bad trade
  3. You lose more
  4. Amygdala activates again
  5. Repeat

The Solution
Systems, Not Willpower
You can't "be more disciplined" when your rational brain is offline.

You need pre-built systems that work even when you can't think straight.

Practical Systems

Revenge Trading Defenses

Mandatory break after losses

Circuit breakers you can't override
