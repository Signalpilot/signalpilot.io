The Framework

Post-Loss Journal Template

After Every Losing Trade, Ask:
1
Was the setup valid?
Did this trade match your criteria, or was it impulsive?
2
Did I follow my rules?
Entry, stop, target — were they all pre-planned?
3
What would I do differently?
Be specific. “I should have waited” isn’t enough.
4
What is the lesson?
Write one sentence: “The lesson here is ___.”
