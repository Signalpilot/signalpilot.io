Slide 4 — The Framework

THE FRAMEWORK
Reading Volume
Step by Step
1
Check the Context
Is price trending, ranging, or at a key level? Volume means different things in different contexts.
2
Compare to Average
Is current volume above or below the 20-period average? Relative volume matters more than absolute.
3
Watch the Reaction
Does price follow through on the volume? High volume with no follow-through = distribution, not conviction.
4
Look for Divergence
When price moves one way and volume moves the other — the volume is telling the truth.
5
Confirm Your Thesis
Volume should support your trade idea. If it contradicts it, respect the volume. It doesn't lie.
COMMON TRAP
Looking at volume in isolation. Volume without price context is meaningless. A spike at resistance means something very different from a spike at support. Always pair volume with price action.

What Volume Really Teaches
Confirmation Power
Volume confirms what price suggests. A breakout without volume is just a wick waiting to happen.

Divergence Is Gold
When price and volume disagree, volume wins. This is the single most reliable volume signal.
Context Is Everything
The same volume spike means different things at different price locations. Always read volume in context.
