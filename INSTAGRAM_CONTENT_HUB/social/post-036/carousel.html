KEY CONCEPT
What Is Absorption?
DEFINITION
When large players quietly accumulate while price falls, absorbing the selling pressure without letting price collapse further. The volume is real. The direction it implies is not what retail thinks.
1
Price Falls on High Volume
Retail traders panic sell. Volume surges as fear takes over. The move looks like it will continue lower.
2
Large Buyers Step In
Institutional players begin quietly absorbing sell orders. They do not chase. They let price come to them and fill slowly.
3
Price Stabilizes
Despite continued selling pressure, price stops falling. The floor holds because absorbed volume equals or exceeds supply.
4
Sellers Exhaust, Price Reverses
Once sellers run out of inventory, buyers take control. Price moves up with conviction as the absorbed supply is depleted.
