Slide 4 — Definition
Key Concept
What Is Absorption?
Definition
When large players quietly accumulate while price falls, absorbing the selling pressure without letting price collapse further.
1
Price falls on high volume. Retail traders panic sell.
2
Large buyers step in quietly, absorbing the sell orders.
3
Price stabilizes despite continued selling pressure.
4
Sellers exhaust. Price reverses as buyers take control.
Slide 5 — Signals
How To Spot It
Signs of Absorption
VOL
High Volume on Down Move
Unusually heavy volume during a selloff suggests large players are active, not just retail panic.
HOLD
Price Stabilizes
Despite selling pressure, price stops falling or narrows into a tight range at support.
WICK
Long Wicks Rejecting Lows
Candles show strong buying off the lows. Each attempt to push lower gets rejected.
REV
Follow-Through to the Upside
Once sellers exhaust, price moves up with conviction. The absorption is confirmed.

One signal alone is a hint. Multiple signals together = high-probability absorption zone.
