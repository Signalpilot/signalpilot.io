Breakout Entry

Enter the moment price breaks through a key resistance or support level. You are trading the momentum of the move as it happens.

Pros
  • Catches strong moves early
  • Clear invalidation point
  • Momentum on your side
Cons
  • False breakouts are common
  • Wider stop placement
  • Requires fast execution

Pullback Entry

Wait for price to retrace after an initial move, then enter at a better price within the trend. You trade the continuation, not the initiation.

Pros
  • Better risk-to-reward
  • Tighter stop loss
  • Trend already confirmed
Cons
  • May miss the move entirely
  • Pullback might not come
  • Requires patience

Retest Entry

After price breaks a level, wait for it to come back and test that level as new support or resistance. The retest confirms the break was genuine.

Pros
  • Highest probability setup
  • Tight, defined stop
  • Confirms structural change
Cons
  • Not all levels get retested
  • Slow to develop
  • Can miss extended moves

Slide 6 — Confirmation Signals
06 / 08

Confirmation
Signals

Never enter blind. Stack these filters to validate your entry.

█
Volume Spike
Above-average volume on the breakout bar confirms participation and conviction behind the move.
●
Candle Close
Wait for the candle to close beyond the level. Wicks lie. Bodies confirm.
▲
Momentum Shift
Check if momentum indicators align with the direction of your entry. Divergence is a warning.
◆
Timeframe Alignment
Your entry timeframe and your bias timeframe should agree. If the daily is bearish, think twice about a 5-min long.
