KEY CONCEPT
Point of Control

The single price level with the most volume traded

POC
🧲
MAGNET EFFECT
Acts as a magnet. Price is pulled back toward POC repeatedly throughout the session.
⚖️
FAIR VALUE
Represents fair value. Where buyers and sellers found the most agreement on price.
🔄
MEAN REVERSION
Anchor point. Price often returns to prior session POC. Use it as a reference level.
