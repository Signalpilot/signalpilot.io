DEFINITION

Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution

A
Phase 1 — Accumulation
The Quiet Range
Smart money builds positions while price consolidates in a tight range. Low volume. Low volatility. Most traders ignore it.
M
Phase 2 — Manipulation
The Fake Move
A false breakout triggers stop losses and lures traders into the wrong direction. This is the trap — engineered liquidity.
D
Phase 3 — Distribution
The Real Move
Price reverses and moves aggressively in the intended direction. Smart money distributes into retail orders created by the manipulation.

THE TRAP

How Manipulation Works

Typical Scenario
The London Open Sweep
01 Asian session creates a clean range between 1.0850 and 1.0870
02 London opens and price breaks below 1.0850 — retail shorts trigger, buyers get stopped out
03 Price quickly reclaims 1.0850, trapping the shorts. The liquidity below has been swept.
04 Distribution begins: price rallies through 1.0870 and beyond with momentum. That was the real direction all along.

IDENTIFICATION

5 Signs AMD is Forming

1 Asian range is tight — less than 30 pips on major pairs. The tighter the range, the stronger the setup.
2 Clean highs or lows — equal highs/lows at range boundaries create obvious liquidity pools that smart money targets.
3 London sweep occurs — price breaks one side of the Asian range, then aggressively reverses within 15-30 minutes.
4 Displacement candle — a large-bodied candle with minimal wicks that confirms the real direction. This is your signal.
5 FVG or breaker forms — the displacement creates an imbalance (fair value gap) that acts as a re-entry zone.

AVOID THESE

4 AMD Mistakes

01
Trading the Manipulation
Entering during the fake move instead of waiting for the reversal. Patience is the edge.
02
No Displacement
Entering before a displacement candle confirms the real direction. Reclaim alone isn't enough.
03
Wrong Session
Trying to find AMD patterns during low-volume hours. This is a session-dependent model.
04
Ignoring HTF Bias
AMD works best when aligned with the higher timeframe trend. Don't fight the daily direction.
