The Imposter Signs

Sound familiar? You’re not alone.
Most traders experience every one of these.

⚠ Warning Signs
✕

Wins feel like flukes — never earned, always accidental

✕

Always waiting to be “exposed” as a fraud

✕

Discounting your progress, no matter the streak

✕

Feeling you don’t deserve success when it comes

Imposter syndrome targets the competent.
If you feel it, you’re closer than you think.

Combat It:
Track Progress

The antidote to doubt is evidence.

✎ Build Your Evidence File
01

Journal your trades objectively — entries, exits, reasoning

02

Note the work behind wins — not just the result

03

See patterns in your improvement across weeks and months

04

Let data speak — it doesn’t lie, and it doesn’t flatter

Key Insight

Your journal is your mirror.

It shows the real you — not the imposter your fear invents.
