The Quote
“Complexity is the enemy of execution. The best strategies are simple enough to follow under pressure.”
— Signal Pilot

Most traders don’t fail because their strategy is wrong. They fail because it’s too complicated to execute when it matters.

The Trap

How Complexity Creeps In

01
Indicator Overload
Adding one more indicator “just in case.” Now your chart is unreadable.
02
Timeframe Paralysis
Checking 6 timeframes for confirmation. By the time you agree with yourself, the move is over.
03
Rule Creep
“Only if RSI is above 50 AND MACD crosses AND volume spikes AND it’s a Tuesday.”
04
Optimisation Addiction
Curve-fitting to perfection on past data. Creating a system that only works in hindsight.

Each addition feels like an improvement. Together, they form a prison.

Framework

Rules That Work Under Pressure

1
One sentence edge
If you can’t describe your edge in a single sentence, it’s too complex.
2
Three conditions max
Entry requires three conditions or fewer. If you need more, you’re compensating.
3
Pre-set the exit
Target and stop defined before the trade. No live decision-making on exits.
4
Two timeframes only
One for direction, one for execution. More creates contradiction, not confirmation.
5
Can you teach it?
If a new trader can’t learn your system in 30 minutes, simplify until they can.

Your Audit

The Simplicity Checklist

  • Can I describe my edge in one sentence?
  • Do I use 3 or fewer entry conditions?
  • Is my stop and target set before entering?
  • Am I looking at 2 timeframes maximum?
  • Could I teach my system to a beginner?
  • Do I execute without hesitation when conditions are met?
  • Is every indicator on my chart there for a specific reason?
  • Would removing one indicator break my system? If not — remove it.
