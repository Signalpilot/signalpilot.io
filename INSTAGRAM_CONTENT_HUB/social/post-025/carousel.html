"Price bounced off the 200 EMA"

Moving averages are lagging calculations, not support/resistance levels.

"Golden Cross = Buy Signal"
By the time it crosses, the move is often over.

The 50/200 crossover happens late in the trend. You're buying the news, not the rumor.

Slide 5 — Truth #1
SIGNALPILOT
The Right Way
Truth #1
MAs Are Trend Filters
Above the 200 EMA? Look for longs.
Below? Look for shorts.

Use MAs to determine directional bias, not entry signals. Filter, not trigger.

Truth #2
Multi-Timeframe Alignment
All MAs pointing the same direction = strong trend.

Daily, 4H, and 1H MAs aligned? High probability setup.

Mixed signals? Stay cautious.

Truth #3
Context, Not Triggers
