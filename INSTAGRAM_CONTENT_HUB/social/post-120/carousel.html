What Is Divergence?

Definition
When price moves in one direction but the indicator moves in the opposite — a disagreement between price action and underlying momentum.
Why It Matters
Divergence signals exhaustion. The trend is running on fumes. Smart traders watch for these moments.

Divergence doesn't predict direction — it predicts a change in conviction.

Two Types of Divergence

BULLISH
Price: Lower Low
Plutus Flow: Higher Low
Signal: Selling pressure weakening
BEARISH
Price: Higher High
Plutus Flow: Lower High
Signal: Buying pressure fading

Always confirm divergence with a key level. Divergence alone is a clue, not a trigger.
