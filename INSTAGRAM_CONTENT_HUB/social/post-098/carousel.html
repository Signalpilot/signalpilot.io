Slide 2 — What to Log

What to Log Every Trade

E
Entry & Exit
Price, time, and the exact reason you entered and exited
S
Setup Type
Which strategy or pattern triggered the trade (e.g. BOS pullback, FVG fill)
R
Risk & R:R
Position size, stop loss, take profit, and actual risk-to-reward achieved
M
Emotional State
How you felt before, during, and after. Were you calm, anxious, or revenge trading?
V
Screenshot
Chart at entry with markup. Your future self will thank you for the visual context.
L
Lesson Learned
One key takeaway — what would you do differently, or what confirmed your edge?

Slide 4 — Weekly Review

The Weekly Review

1
Count Your Trades
Were you overtrading or undertrading relative to your plan this week?
2
Calculate Win Rate
What was your actual win rate? Compare it to your long-term average.
3
Find Patterns
Which setups won? Which lost? What time of day worked best?
4
Set One Goal
Pick one thing to improve next week. Not five. One measurable target.
The weekly review converts raw data into actionable intelligence.
