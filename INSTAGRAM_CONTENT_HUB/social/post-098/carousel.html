Slide 2 — What to Log

What to Log Every Trade

1
Entry & Exit
Price, time, and the exact reason you entered and exited
2
Setup Type
Which strategy or pattern triggered the trade (e.g. BOS pullback, FVG fill)
3
Risk & R:R
Position size, stop loss, take profit, and actual risk-to-reward achieved
4
Emotional State
How you felt before, during, and after. Were you calm, anxious, or revenge trading?
5
Screenshot
Chart at entry with markup. Your future self will thank you for the visual context.
6
Lesson Learned
One key takeaway — what would you do differently, or what confirmed your edge?

Slide 4 — Weekly Review

The Weekly Review

01
Count Your Trades
How many trades this week? Were you overtrading or undertrading relative to your plan?
02
Calculate Win Rate
What was your actual win rate this week? Compare it to your long-term average.
03
Find Patterns
Which setups won? Which lost? What time of day worked best? Look for recurring themes.
04
Set One Goal
Pick one thing to improve next week. Not five. One single, measurable improvement.
