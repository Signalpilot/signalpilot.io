3 Types of Structure

Bullish Structure
Higher highs + higher lows. Buyers in control. Look for longs.
Bearish Structure
Lower highs + lower lows. Sellers in control. Look for shorts.
Ranging Structure
Equal highs + equal lows. No dominant side. Wait or fade extremes.

Slide 3 — Key Terms

Key Terms

HH / HL
Higher High / Higher Low — bullish trend markers
LH / LL
Lower High / Lower Low — bearish trend markers
Swing Point
A significant high or low where price reversed
Structure Break
When price violates the last significant swing point

Slide 4 — BOS vs CHoCH
04 / 06

BOS vs CHoCH

BOS
Break of Structure
Trend continuation. Price breaks the last swing in the direction of the trend.
CHoCH
Change of Character
Trend reversal. Price breaks the last swing against the current trend.
