3 Types of Structure

▲
Bullish Structure
HH + HL → HH + HL
Higher highs and higher lows. Buyers are in control. Look for long entries on pullbacks to higher lows.
TREND STRENGTH — 88%
▼
Bearish Structure
LH + LL → LH + LL
Lower highs and lower lows. Sellers dominate. Look for short entries on rallies to lower highs.
TREND STRENGTH — 85%
▬
Ranging Structure
EH + EL ↔ EH + EL
Equal highs and equal lows. No dominant side. Wait for a break or fade the extremes.
TREND STRENGTH — 45%

Slide 3 — Key Terms

Key Terms

▲
HH / HL
Higher High / Higher Low — the signature of bullish structure. Each swing pushes higher than the last.
▼
LH / LL
Lower High / Lower Low — bearish structure confirmed. Each rally fails lower than the previous.
◆
Swing Point
A significant high or low where price reversed. The building blocks of all market structure.
⚡
Structure Break
When price violates the last significant swing point — the moment trend may shift or accelerate.

Slide 4 — BOS vs CHoCH
04 / 06

BOS vs CHoCH

→
BOS
Break of Structure
Trend continuation. Price breaks the last swing in the direction of the existing trend.
CONTINUATION — 75%
↺
CHoCH
Change of Character
Trend reversal signal. Price breaks the last swing against the current trend direction.
REVERSAL — 65%

BOS confirms. CHoCH warns. Know the difference.
