```
Mean Reversion Trading 📊
```

**Type:** EDU | **Pillar:** P3: Market Mechanics

### Twitter/X Copy
```
Market structure: The skeleton of price.

Higher highs + higher lows = Uptrend
Lower highs + lower lows = Downtrend
Neither = Range

Everything else is built on this foundation.
Master structure before indicators.

#MarketStructure #TradingEducation #SignalPilot #TechnicalAnalysis #Basics
```

### Instagram Caption
```
Market Structure Fundamentals

THE SKELETON OF PRICE

UPTREND STRUCTURE:
Higher highs (HH)
Higher lows (HL)
Demand in control
Look for longs

DOWNTREND STRUCTURE:
Lower highs (LH)
Lower lows (LL)
Supply in control
Look for shorts

RANGING STRUCTURE:
Equal highs
Equal lows (roughly)
Neither side winning
Trade range or wait

STRUCTURE BREAKS:

BREAK OF STRUCTURE (BOS):
Continuation signal
HH in uptrend = BOS
LL in downtrend = BOS
Trend continues

CHANGE OF CHARACTER (CHOCH):
Reversal signal
LL in uptrend = CHOCH
HH in downtrend = CHOCH
Trend may reverse

WHY IT MATTERS:
Structure is context for everything
Indicators mean nothing without structure
Trade WITH structure, not against
```
