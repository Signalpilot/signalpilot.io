The Secret

Subtlety is everything.
Teal gradient fades from LEFT edge → transparent.
Orange gradient fades from transparent → RIGHT edge.
Colors at only 10% opacity. Borders at 60% opacity.

Only SLIDE 1 needs this treatment. Slides 2-10 can be any style.

How to Build in Canva

Left Column (Teal)

1 Set background to #0c0c0c
2 Add rectangle, fill with gradient: #14B8A6 → transparent at 10% opacity
3 Add thin line on LEFT edge only, 2px, color #14B8A6 at 60% opacity
4 Text: light teal tint or white

Center Column (Neutral)

1 Set background to #080808
2 Optional: very subtle white gradient at 2% opacity
3 NO colored borders
4 Text: #E5E5E5 (soft white)

Right Column (Orange)

1 Set background to #0c0c0c
2 Add rectangle, fill with gradient: transparent → #FB923C at 10% opacity
3 Add thin line on RIGHT edge only, 2px, color #FB923C at 60% opacity
4 Text: light orange tint or white
