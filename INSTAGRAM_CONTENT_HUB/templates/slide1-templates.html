How to Recreate in Canva

Step 1: Create new design → Instagram Post (1080 × 1080 px)

Step 2: Set background color to #0c0c0c

Step 3 (Teal/Orange only): Add a rectangle covering the full canvas. Apply gradient:

• Teal: #14B8A6 → transparent, direction: top-left to bottom-right, opacity 10%
• Orange: transparent → #FB923C, direction: top-left to bottom-right, opacity 10%

Step 4 (Teal/Orange only): Add a thin line (2px) on the edge:

• Teal: LEFT edge, color #14B8A6 at 60% opacity
• Orange: RIGHT edge, color #FB923C at 60% opacity

Step 5: Add your text. Use Inter or Montserrat font.

• Title: 48-64px, bold
• Subtitle: 20-24px, regular
• Label: 12-14px, uppercase, letter-spacing

Step 6: Add SignalPilot logo at bottom (very subtle, 10-15% opacity)
