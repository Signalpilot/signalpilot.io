# Outreach Audit — Rating & Review (75 Emails)

> Scoring criteria (1-10 per category):
> 1. **Hook** — Does the opener grab attention? Is it specific to THEM?
> 2. **Brevity** — Is it short enough? Would a busy person read the whole thing?
> 3. **Indicator Fit** — Are the 2 indicators genuinely matched to their philosophy?
> 4. **Tone** — Does it feel like trader-to-trader, or company-to-influencer?
> 5. **Affiliate Smoothness** — Is the affiliate mention an afterthought or a sales pitch?
> 6. **CTA Clarity** — Is "send your TradingView username" clear and low-friction?
> 7. **Overall Reply Probability** — Would this person actually respond?
>
> Scale: 1-3 = Weak, 4-6 = Decent, 7-8 = Strong, 9-10 = Excellent

## PATTERN ANALYSIS: Why Brian Shannon Worked

The highest-scored emails share these traits:
- **Specific knowledge of their work** (not "I love your content" but "your November ASFX VWAP Deep Dive")
- **Indicator-philosophy alignment** (Pentarch → cycle analysis, Volume Oracle → Wyckoff, Harmonic Oscillator → multi-indicator confirmation)
- **Genuine curiosity** ("I'd be curious how it compares to your methodology" vs "please review our product")
- **Affiliate as afterthought** (one sentence, often with "but your honest take comes first")
- **No overselling** (2 indicators max, 1-line descriptions, no jargon dumps)
- **Clear CTA** ("send your TradingView username" — one simple action)

The lowest-scored emails share these anti-patterns:
- Generic openers ("your channel is great")
- Too many indicators (3+)
- Recipient too large (1M+ subs = won't read cold emails)
- Poor platform fit (pitching TradingView tools to someone who uses a different platform)
- Affiliate mention feels corporate rather than casual
