📊 The Elite Seven Indicators

📌 What is Pentarch?
Pentarch is our flagship indicator for complete trend regime detection. It maps 5-phase market reversal sequences:
  • TD (Touchdown) — Early-cycle reversal on selling exhaustion
  • IGN (Ignition) — Breakout confirmation with conviction
  • WRN (Warning) — Early weakness in uptrends
  • CAP (Climax) — Late-cycle exhaustion with volume spikes
  • BDN (Breakdown) — Bearish structure break
Integrates 4 layers: Regime bars, Pilot Line, NanoFlow Crosses, and Event Signals—all non-repainting. Read Pentarch's origin story →
📌 What is OmniDeck?
OmniDeck is an all-in-one overlay unifying 10 premium systems: Exhaustion Counter, Squeeze Cloud, Bull Market Support Band, SuperTrend Ensemble, Regime System, Supply/Demand Zones, Candlestick Patterns, Liquidity Sweeps, EMA Events, and Caution/Danger Warnings. Each component can be toggled independently for customized analysis. Discover OmniDeck's story →
📌 What is Volume Oracle?
Volume Oracle tracks accumulation/distribution patterns in real-time. Shows volume flow with strength percentage (confidence level), duration tracking, early warning flash before regime flips, and built-in position calculator with risk levels. Discover the Prophet's power →
📌 What is Plutus Flow?
Plutus Flow is a cumulative delta ribbon revealing hidden buying/selling pressure. Green ribbon = buyers control, red ribbon = sellers control. Centerline crosses show regime shifts. White dots mark extreme accumulation/distribution levels. Divergence marks reveal broken trends before price confirms—price can fake a move, volume can't. Explore the Scales of balance →
📌 What is Janus Atlas?
Janus Atlas auto-plots key reference points: HTF levels (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly), previous session data, VWAP anchors, volume profile zones (POC, VAH, VAL), session markers (Asia, London, NY), and structure labels (BOS, CHoCH). All levels update automatically across multiple timeframes. Learn the Cartographer's craft →
📌 What is Augury Grid?
Augury Grid is a multi-symbol watchlist tracking 8 tickers simultaneously with live table showing signal direction (↑ Bullish / ↓ Bearish), time since signal, target prices, quality score (0-100 confluence rating), and running P&L. See which markets have cleanest setups instantly. Meet the Watchman's vigil →
📌 What is Harmonic Oscillator?
Harmonic Oscillator is a 7-oscillator voting system with star rating confidence (★ to ★★★★). Each oscillator votes Bull/Bear/Neutral on every bar. Only signals when multiple oscillators agree. 7/7 agreement = ★★★★ stars = all aligned. Trading rule: One oscillator wrong is common. Seven agreeing is different. Experience the Arbiter's judgment →
📌 Can I use multiple indicators together?
Absolutely! The Elite Seven are designed to work together. Popular combos: Pentarch + Volume Oracle for cycle timing with volume flow confirmation, OmniDeck + Janus Atlas for complete market structure with key levels, or Harmonic Oscillator + Plutus Flow for momentum confirmation with hidden divergences.
