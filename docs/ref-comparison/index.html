    Indicator Comparison Guide¶

    🎯 Quick Selection Guide
    PentarchReversal Detection — Exhaustion, Cycle Phases, Exhaustion Counter
    OmniDeckTrend Following — All-in-One, 10 Integrated Systems
    Volume OracleVolume Analysis — Money Flow, Position Tracking
    Janus AtlasLevels & Structure — 60+ Level Types, Support/Resistance
    Harmonic OscillatorMomentum Consensus — 7-Component Voting, Divergence

    Pick the indicator that matches your primary trading goal.

    Match your Signal Pilot indicator selection to your trading style and experience level. This guide presents the comparison framework.

    🎯 Quick Recommendations¶

    👶 Complete Beginner¶

    Start with: Pentarch only

    Why: - Simplest to understand (5 clear signals) - Focused on one thing: reversals - Not overwhelming - Works on any market/timeframe

    Learning path: Master Pentarch first (2-4 weeks), then add others

    📈 Beginner with Some Experience¶

    Start with: OmniDeck

    Why: - All-in-one solution (includes Pentarch + more) - Everything on one clean chart - No need to manage multiple indicators - Complete trading system

    Alternative: Pentarch + Janus Atlas (signals + levels)

    💼 Intermediate Trader¶

    Recommended setup: - Pentarch - Core signals - Janus Atlas - Key levels - Volume Oracle OR Harmonic Oscillator - Confirmation

    Why: - Confluence between signals and levels - Volume or momentum confirmation - Custom workflow flexibility

    🏆 Advanced Trader¶

    Custom combinations based on strategy: - Build your own stack - Mix and match based on market conditions - Use Augury Grid for multi-symbol scanning - Leverage all 7 indicators strategically

    📝 3-Question Quiz¶

    Question 1: What's your experience level?

    A) Complete beginner - Never used technical indicators before → Recommended: Janus Atlas or Volume Oracle

    B) Some experience - Used RSI, MACD, moving averages → Recommended: Pentarch or The Trinity

    C) Advanced - Comfortable with complex multi-indicator systems → Recommended: OmniDeck or full Signal Pilot suite

    Question 2: What's your primary trading style?

    A) Scalping (seconds to minutes, many trades per day) → Recommended: Volume Oracle + Harmonic Oscillator → Timeframes: 1m-5m

    B) Day Trading (minutes to hours, close all by EOD) → Recommended: The Trinity (Pentarch + Janus + Volume) → Timeframes: 5m-1H

    C) Swing Trading (days to weeks) → Recommended: Pentarch + Janus Atlas → Timeframes: 4H-Daily

    D) Position Trading (weeks to months) → Recommended: Pentarch + Plutus Flow → Timeframes: Daily-Weekly

    Question 3: What's your primary goal?

    A) Find precise entry/exit levels → Recommended: Janus Atlas (60+ price levels)

    B) Identify market cycle phase → Recommended: Pentarch (5 cycle events)

    C) Confirm moves with volume → Recommended: Volume Oracle (regime detection, BULL/BEAR signals)

    D) Get momentum signals → Recommended: Harmonic Oscillator (BUY/SELL/STRONG/NEUT)

    E) Spot divergences → Recommended: Plutus Flow (OBV divergence detection)

    F) Scan multiple assets quickly → Recommended: Augury Grid (multi-symbol screener)

    G) All of the above → Recommended: OmniDeck (8-in-1 indicator)

    🔍 Detailed Indicator Profiles¶

    Pentarch - Cycle Phase Detection¶

    What it does: Detects potential reversal points with 5 distinct signal types (TD, IGN, WRN, CAP, BDN)

    Best for: - Multi-day users - Reversal-focused users - Trend users looking for pullback entries - Beginners learning price action

    Strengths: - Clear, unambiguous signals - Works on all timeframes - Simple to understand - Alert-ready

    Limitations: - Doesn't show support/resistance levels - No volume or momentum context - Can produce signals at poor levels

    Standalone usage: Commonly used alone by beginners or reversal-focused users

    Common pairing: Janus Atlas (for level confirmation)

    May be skipped: By users focused exclusively on breakouts or continuations

    Janus Atlas - Multi-Timeframe Auto-Levels¶

    What it does: Identifies key price levels: support, resistance, FVG, order blocks, structure breaks, plus Opening Range, Gap Levels, Killzones, Confluence Zones, and auto-drawn Fibonacci retracement levels

    Best for: - Level-based users - Range users - Breakout users - Session/killzone users - Anyone who uses support/resistance

    Strengths: - Shows WHERE to enter (60+ level types) - Works in ranging and trending markets - Visual and easy to understand - Great for confluence with other indicators - Killzones highlight optimal trading windows - Distance Table shows proximity to levels - Fibonacci levels with 19 anchor options - 61 configurable alerts

    Limitations: - Doesn't provide timing signals (levels only) - Requires price action understanding - Can display many levels (important ones are commonly filtered)

    Standalone usage: Commonly used alone by experienced level-based users

    Common pairing: Pentarch (for entry timing at levels)

    May be skipped: By users who don't incorporate levels in their strategy

    OmniDeck - Unified Chart Overlay¶

    What it does: Combines ALL suite indicators into one comprehensive dashboard

    Includes: - Pentarch reversal signals - Janus Atlas levels - Volume Oracle volume - Harmonic Oscillator momentum - Bias indicators - Market regime filters

    Best for: - Traders who want complete analysis - Those who don't want multiple indicators - Intermediate to advanced users - Full-system users

    Strengths: - Everything in one place - Clean, organized display - No need to manage multiple indicators - Complete trading system

    Limitations: - Can be overwhelming for beginners - More complex settings - Busy on smaller screens - Requires understanding all components

    Standalone usage: Designed as complete, all-in-one solution

    Common pairing: None—includes all functionality

    May be skipped: By users preferring simple, focused indicators

    Augury Grid - Multi-Timeframe Scanner¶

    What it does: Scans multiple symbols simultaneously, showing signals in a table format

    Best for: - Multi-day users with watchlists - Multi-market users - Opportunity hunters - Portfolio managers

    Strengths: - See entire watchlist at once - Quickly find setup across markets - Saves time vs checking each chart - Customizable symbol lists

    Limitations: - Doesn't show chart details - Still need to open charts to trade - Limited to TradingView symbol limits - Table may be large with many symbols

    Standalone usage: Designed for scanning opportunities

    Common pairing: Pentarch or OmniDeck on individual charts

    May be skipped: By users focusing on 1-2 symbols

    Volume Oracle - Regime Detection¶

    What it does: Analyzes order flow and volume patterns for confirmation

    Best for: - Volume-focused users - Confluence seekers - Traders who use volume analysis - Breakout users (volume confirmation)

    Strengths: - Volume-based confirmation - Reduces false signals - Great for breakout validation - Shows volume flow activity

    Limitations: - Not a standalone entry system - Requires understanding of volume - Best as confirmation tool - Crypto volumes may be misleading

    Standalone usage: Not designed for standalone use—requires price signals

    Common pairing: Pentarch + Janus (signal + level + volume)

    May be skipped: By users who don't incorporate volume in strategy

    Harmonic Oscillator - Multi-Component Momentum¶

    What it does: Combines multiple oscillators into one momentum indicator

    Best for: - Momentum users - Divergence users - Overbought/oversold seekers - Trend confirmation

    Strengths: - Smoother than single oscillators - Great for divergence detection - Momentum confirmation - Trend strength measurement

    Limitations: - Not a standalone entry system - Can lag in fast markets - Requires interpretation - Best as confirmation

    Standalone usage: Not designed for standalone use—requires price signals

    Common pairing: Pentarch (signal + momentum confirmation)

    May be skipped: By users who don't use momentum indicators

    Plutus Flow - Statistical OBV Analysis¶

    What it does: Enhanced On-Balance Volume indicator for volume trend analysis

    Best for: - Volume trend users - Divergence hunters - Confirmation seekers - Long-term trend users

    Strengths: - Clearer than standard OBV - Excellent divergence signals - Trend confirmation - Works on all timeframes

    Limitations: - Not a standalone entry system - Crypto volume issues - Best as confirmation - Requires volume understanding

    Standalone usage: Not designed for standalone use—requires price signals

    Common pairing: Pentarch + Janus

    May be skipped: By users who don't incorporate volume in strategy

    🤔 Decision Tree: Which Indicators Do I Need?¶

    Question 1: Trading experience level?¶

    • Brand new to trading: Pentarch only commonly recommended

      Initial mastery period: 2-4 weeks, then guide revisitation

    • Some experience present: Question 2 applies

    Question 2: All-in-one preference?¶

    • Single comprehensive indicator preferred: OmniDeck is appropriate

      Other indicators not required

    • Custom setup preferred: Question 3 applies

    Question 3: Reversal or breakout focus?¶

    • Reversal focus: Pentarch commonly essential
    • Breakout focus: Janus Atlas commonly essential
    • Both approaches: Both Pentarch + Janus typically used

    Question 4: Volume incorporation?¶

    • Volume used: Volume Oracle commonly added
    • Volume divergence focus: Plutus Flow commonly added
    • Volume not used: Volume indicators typically skipped

    Question 5: Momentum/oscillator usage?¶

    • Momentum used: Harmonic Oscillator commonly added
    • Momentum not used: Typically skipped

    Question 6: Multi-symbol trading?¶

    • 5+ symbols: Augury Grid commonly added for scanning
    • 1-3 symbols: Grid typically skipped, charts checked individually

    💡 Common Mistakes¶

    ❌ Using Too Many Indicators¶

    Problem: All 7 indicators on one chart Issue: Overwhelming, slow, analysis paralysis Fix: Commonly recommended maximum of 3 indicators per chart

    ❌ Using Confirmation Without Signals¶

    Problem: Only using Volume Oracle or Harmonic Oscillator Issue: No entry triggers Fix: A signal indicator (Pentarch or OmniDeck) provides the foundation

    ❌ Ignoring Key Levels¶

    Problem: Taking Pentarch signals anywhere Issue: Many false signals away from levels Fix: Add Janus Atlas to identify key levels where signals historically show different characteristics

    ❌ Switching Indicators Too Fast¶

    Problem: Changing setup every week Issue: Mastery becomes difficult Fix: Sticking with one setup for at least 3 months is commonly recommended

    ❌ Using OmniDeck + Individual Indicators¶

    Problem: OmniDeck + Pentarch + Janus all loaded Issue: Duplicate signals, redundant data Fix: Choose OmniDeck OR individual indicators, but not both

    📈 By Trading Style¶

    Day Traders¶

    Primary: Pentarch Add: Janus Atlas Optional: Volume Oracle Timeframes: 15m, 1H

    Swing Traders¶

    Primary: Pentarch Add: Janus Atlas Optional: Harmonic Oscillator Timeframes: 4H, Daily

    Scalpers¶

    Primary: OmniDeck (speed) Timeframes: 1m, 5m, 15m Note: Need fast decision-making

    Position Traders¶

    Primary: OmniDeck OR Pentarch + Janus Optional: Plutus Flow Timeframes: Daily, Weekly

    Range Traders¶

    Primary: Janus Atlas (for levels) Add: Pentarch (for entries at levels) Note: Focus on support/resistance bounces

    Breakout Traders¶

    Primary: Janus Atlas (for levels to break) Add: Volume Oracle (volume confirmation) Note: Wait for volume on breaks

    🎓 Learning Path¶

    Week 1-2: Foundation¶

    • Learn Pentarch signals
    • Practice identifying TD, IGN, WRN, CAP, BDN
    • Paper trade with Pentarch only
    • Goal: Understand all 5 signal types

    Week 3-4: Confirmation¶

    • Add Janus Atlas
    • Learn to identify key levels
    • Trade Pentarch signals only AT Janus levels
    • Goal: Understand confluence

    Week 5-6: Refinement¶

    • Add ONE confirmation indicator:
    • Volume Oracle (if volume is liked)
    • Harmonic Oscillator (if momentum is liked)
    • Goal: Filter for highest probability setups

    Week 7-8: Efficiency¶

    • Add Augury Grid if trading multiple symbols
    • Streamline your workflow
    • Goal: Fast scanning, confident execution

    Week 9+: Mastery¶

    • Consistent results with your setup
    • Consider OmniDeck if you want simplicity
    • Or stick with your working combination
    • Goal: Profitable, repeatable process
