    Volume Oracle v1.0 Quick Reference

    One-page cheat sheet for regime detection, signals, and risk management

    🎯 The 5 Systems

    System Display Purpose Key Features
    Regime Detection 🟩🟥 Background + Meter Market Phase Green = Accumulation, Red = Distribution, Gray = Neutral, Gold = Weakening. Regime Meter shows state, strength, health, confirmation counter, flow, duration
    Signal Generation ⬆️⬇️ BULL/BEAR Labels Event Detection Signals with ⭐⭐⭐ quality ratings (80-100%, 60-79%, 40-59%)
    Risk Management 📍 Price Lines Trade Levels Auto-calculated Entry, Stop, T1, T2 with position sizing
    HTF Confirmation ✅❌ Alignment Status Multi-Timeframe Checks if higher timeframe regime agrees with signal
    Strategy Modes ⚙️ Mode Setting Trading Approach Trend Following, Mean Reversion, or Hybrid (default)

    📊 Signal Quality Ratings

    Rating Quality % Meaning Action
    ⭐⭐⭐ 80-100% High Quality Strong confluence → Full position size, high confidence
    ⭐⭐ 60-79% Medium Quality Good setup → Moderate position size
    ⭐ 40-59% Lower Quality Caution → Reduced size or skip

    ⚡ Quick Interpretation Guide

    Regime Colors

    • Green background: Accumulation regime → Favor BULL signals
    • Red background: Distribution regime → Favor BEAR signals
    • Gray background: Neutral regime → Wait for direction or be selective
    • Gold background: Weakening regime → Protect profits, tighten stops

    Regime Meter Table (6 Rows)

    RowWhat It ShowsExample
    StateCurrent regimeAccumulation, Distribution, Neutral
    StrengthHow strong (0-100%)78%
    HealthWarning count (0/1/2/3+)🟢 HEALTHY, 🟡 WATCH, 🟠 CAUTION, 🔴 DANGER
    ConfirmBars building↑4 (4 bars)
    FlowBuy/sell directionBullish, Bearish, Mixed
    DurationHow long in regime23 bars

    Regime Status (Early Warnings)

    • ✓ STRONG: All good → Trade normally
    • 📉 FADING: Slowing down → Tighten stops
    • ⚡ WEAKENING: May flip soon → Reduce size
    • ⚠️ CRITICAL: About to flip → Consider exit

    Warning types: Price slowing, Buyers/sellers mixed, Volume dropping

    Volume Bar Coloring

    • Bright Green: Strong buyers (>70%)
    • Soft Green: Moderate buyers (50-70%)
    • Bright Red: Strong sellers (>70%)
    • Soft Red: Moderate sellers (50-70%)
    • Gold: Weakening (be careful)
    • Gray: No clear direction

    HTF Alignment

    • BULL + HTF Aligned: Higher TF also in Accumulation → Higher probability
    • BEAR + HTF Aligned: Higher TF also in Distribution → Higher probability
    • Signal + HTF Not Aligned: Conflicting timeframes → Lower probability, reduce size

    🤝 Best Combinations

    The Trinity (Highest Probability)

    1. ✅ Pentarch cycle signal (TD, IGN, CAP, etc.)
    2. ✅ Janus Atlas level within 1-2%
    3. ✅ Volume Oracle BULL/BEAR ⭐⭐⭐ signal with HTF aligned

    Example: Pentarch TD at Daily Low + BULL ⭐⭐⭐ + HTF Aligned = Very high probability setup

    With Janus Atlas

    • BULL signal at Daily/Weekly Low = Strong support confluence
    • BEAR signal at Daily/Weekly High = Strong resistance confluence
    • Accumulation regime + Janus Level = High-probability reversal area

    With Plutus Flow

    • Bullish divergence + BULL signal = Confirmation of reversal
    • Bearish divergence + BEAR signal = Confirmation of top

    ⚙️ Recommended Settings

    Scalping (1m-5m charts)

    • Strategy Mode: Trend Following
    • Focus on: ⭐⭐⭐ signals only
    • HTF: Check 1H alignment

    Day Trading (15m-1H charts)

    • Strategy Mode: Hybrid
    • Focus on: ⭐⭐ and ⭐⭐⭐ signals
    • Risk Amount: 1% per trade

    Swing Trading (4H-Daily charts)

    • Strategy Mode: Trend Following
    • Focus on: Regime strength >70%
    • HTF: Daily/Weekly alignment

    ⚠️ When NOT to Use

    ❌ Pre-market/after-hours (low volume distorts readings)
    ❌ Low volume assets (<$100K daily volume)
    ❌ Crypto with fake volume (wash trading)
    ❌ First 5 minutes of market open (volume settling)

    🔧 Troubleshooting

    "Too many signals firing"
    → Increase quality threshold (focus on ⭐⭐⭐ only)
    → Use Trend Following mode instead of Hybrid
    → Wait for HTF alignment before trading

    "Regime changing frequently"
    → Normal during choppy markets
    → Consider higher timeframe for clearer regimes
    → Wait for regime strength >60% before trading

    "Signals not appearing"
    → Check that signals are enabled in settings
    → Verify regime is established (not gray/neutral)
    → Lower quality threshold if too restrictive

    📊 Timeframe Suitability

    Timeframe Suitability Notes
    1m-5m ✅ Good Scalping, focus on ⭐⭐⭐ signals only
    15m ✅ Excellent Day trading, all systems work well
    1H ⭐ Best Day/swing trading sweet spot
    4H ⭐ Best Swing trading, clearest regime detection
    Daily ✅ Excellent Position trading, high-quality signals
    Weekly ✅ Good Long-term, regime very reliable

    🎯 Volume Oracle Success Tips

    1. Regime First, Signal Second — Check regime color before acting on signals
    2. Quality Matters — ⭐⭐⭐ signals have highest success rate
    3. Check Health Status — Monitor for FADING/WEAKENING/CRITICAL warnings early
    4. HTF Alignment = Confidence — Signals with HTF alignment are more reliable
    5. Combine with Janus levels — Signal at key level = highest probability
    6. Watch Confirmation Counter — ↑4+ building = regime change may be coming

    📖 Key Concepts

    Regime Detection: Background colors showing market phase (Accumulation/Distribution/Neutral/Weakening)
    Regime Meter: 6-row table (State, Strength, Health, Confirm, Flow, Duration) for complete regime info
    Regime Status: Leading indicators (STRONG/FADING/WEAKENING/CRITICAL) that warn before exits trigger
    Confirmation Counter: Shows consecutive bars building toward regime change (↑4 = 4 bars confirming)
    Event Detection: BULL/BEAR events with quality ratings (⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐)
    Risk Management: Auto-calculated Entry, Stop, T1, T2 with position sizing
    HTF Confirmation: Higher timeframe regime alignment check for signal validation
