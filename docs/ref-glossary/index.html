    Signal Pilot Glossary¶

    Complete reference of all Signal Pilot indicator terms, signals, and visual elements. Updated December 2025 from official documentation.

    🎯 Pentarch — Five Cycle Events¶

    Close-confirmed cycle detection across accumulation, markup, distribution, climax, and decline phases.

    🟣 TD — Touchdown¶

    Color: Purple 🟣
    Position: Label below candle
    Phase: Accumulation — Quiet buying after decline
    Meaning: Downward exhaustion detected, early cycle warning
    Indicates: Accumulation phase conditions detected

    🩵 IGN — Ignition¶

    Color: Teal 🩵
    Position: Label below candle
    Phase: Markup — Prices climbing as more buyers join
    Meaning: Upward momentum starting, momentum confirmation
    Indicates: Early-cycle momentum conditions detected

    🟡 WRN — Warning¶

    Color: Yellow 🟡
    Position: Label above candle
    Phase: Distribution — Selling begins near top
    Meaning: Price getting stretched, weakening momentum
    Indicates: Late-cycle weakening conditions detected

    🟠 CAP — Climax¶

    Color: Orange 🟠
    Position: Label above candle
    Phase: Climax — Peak before reversal
    Meaning: Upward exhaustion detected, late-cycle warning
    Indicates: Late-cycle exhaustion conditions detected

    🔴 BDN — Breakdown¶

    Color: Red 🔴
    Position: Label above candle
    Phase: Decline — Prices fall as sellers dominate
    Meaning: Downward pressure detected, bearish breakdown
    Indicates: Markdown phase conditions detected

    Pilot Line¶

    Visual: Thick colored line with semi-transparent ribbon
    Calculation: Double-smoothed EMA (34-period EMA → 3-period EMA)
    Colors:
    • 🟢 Green: Strong uptrend (rising slope)
    • 🔴 Red: Strong downtrend (falling slope)
    • 🟠 Orange: Transitional phase (flat/easing)
    Usage: Price above green = bullish bias, price below red = bearish bias

    Regime (Bar Colors)¶

    Visual: Candle body colors
    Determination: 3-factor voting (2 of 3 required)
    Factors: EMA structure, Price vs Pilot Line, Slope direction
    Colors:
    • 🟢 Green: Bull regime (2+ bull votes)
    • 🔴 Red: Bear regime (2+ bear votes)
    • ⚪ Gray: Neutral/transitional

    NanoFlow¶

    Visual: Small cross marks (green below lows, red above highs)
    Frequency: 10-30 per day on 1H (vs 3-8 main events)
    Purpose: Micro-momentum shifts between main signals
    Note: Confirmation tool only — NOT a standalone trade signal

    💧 Plutus Flow — Advanced OBV¶

    Enhanced On-Balance Volume with trend ribbons, divergence detection, and extreme zone signals.

    OBV Line & Trend Ribbon¶

    OBV Line: Cumulative volume direction (add on up days, subtract on down days)
    Ribbon: Shaded area between OBV and 20-SMA basis
    • 🟢 Green ribbon: OBV above basis = bullish accumulation
    • 🔴 Red ribbon: OBV below basis = bearish distribution

    Cross Signals¶

    🟢 Green dot: OBV crosses ABOVE moving average — bullish momentum shift
    🔴 Red dot: OBV crosses BELOW moving average — bearish momentum shift

    ⚪ Breach Signals (White Dots)¶

    Visual: White dots (3px)
    Meaning: Flow ENTERED extreme zone (±2σ standard deviation)
    • Breach +2σ = Entered overbought zone
    • Breach -2σ = Entered oversold zone
    Action: WARNING — prepare for potential reversal, don't act immediately

    🟡 Zone Exits (Yellow Dots)¶

    Visual: Yellow dots (2px)
    Meaning: Flow LEFT extreme zone (exiting ±2σ)
    • Exit +2σ = Left overbought → reversal conditions present
    • Exit -2σ = Left oversold → reversal conditions present
    Indicates: Extreme condition has ended, reversal phase may be beginning

    Divergence Labels¶

    Bull Div: Price makes lower low, OBV makes higher low → reversal UP likely
    Bear Div: Price makes higher high, OBV makes lower high → reversal DOWN likely
    Bull Hid: Hidden bullish divergence → trend continuation UP
    Bear Hid: Hidden bearish divergence → trend continuation DOWN

    📊 Volume Oracle — Volume Intelligence¶

    5-system volume intelligence: Regime Detection, Signal Generation, Risk Management, HTF Confirmation, Strategy Modes.

    System 1: Regime Detection¶

    Regime States:
    • Accumulation (Green background) — Buying pressure, bullish
    • Distribution (Red background) — Selling pressure, bearish
    • Neutral (Gray) — No clear direction
    • Weakening (Gold) — Current regime losing strength

    Health Status: ✓ HEALTHY → 📉 FADING → ⚡ WEAKENING → ⚠️ CRITICAL
    Confirmation Counter: ↑4 = 4 bars building toward accumulation, ↓2 = 2 bars toward distribution
    Regime Meter Table: State, Strength (0-100%), Health, Confirm, Flow, Duration

    System 2: Signal Generation¶

    Signal Types:
    • 🟢 BULL (↑) — Bullish conditions confirmed
    • 🔴 BEAR (↓) — Bearish conditions confirmed

    Quality Ratings:
    • ⭐⭐⭐ 80-100% — Elite quality, highest confidence
    • ⭐⭐ 60-79% — Premium quality, good confidence
    • ⭐ 40-59% — Standard quality, moderate confidence

    System 3: Risk Management¶

    Auto-Calculated: Position Size, Stop Loss (ATR-based), Target 1 (T1), Target 2 (T2)
    Features: Trailing Stop, Breakeven after T1, Partial Exit %

    System 4: HTF Confirmation¶

    Purpose: Checks if higher timeframe agrees with signal direction
    Display: HTF Table showing regime state, strength %, alignment status

    System 5: Strategy Modes¶

    Trend Following: Signals align with regime direction — for trending markets
    Mean Reversion: Signals at regime extremes — for range-bound markets
    Hybrid: Auto-adapts based on market conditions

    🎵 Harmonic Oscillator — Composite Momentum¶

    Five-oscillator consensus system with voting-based signals.

    The 5 Voting Components¶

    1. MACD Vote — Fires when MACD crosses signal line
    2. RSI Vote — Triple RSI envelope (7/14/28 periods)
    3. Stochastic RSI Vote — K/D crossovers in extreme zones
    4. Volume Vote — Volume delta patterns
    5. Divergence Vote — Price/oscillator divergences

    Signal Types¶

    🟢 Bull: 3+ components vote bullish — green label below candle
    🔴 Bear: 3+ components vote bearish — red label above candle
    ⚡ STRONG: 6-7 components agree — bright label, high confidence
    ⚪ NEUT: 0-2 votes — gray label, no clear consensus

    🎮 OmniDeck — Everything Indicator¶

    All-in-one trading system combining 10 professional analysis tools.

    The 10 Systems¶

    1. Exhaustion Counter — Exhaustion counter (Green 9 = oversold, Red 9 = overbought)
    2. Squeeze — Breakout detector (red dots building → teal/red arrow breakout)
    3. Liquidity Sweeps — Stop hunt detection (💧 = bull sweep, 🩸 = bear sweep)
    4. EMA Trio — Moving averages (50/100/200 EMA)
    5. SuperTrend — Adaptive trend ribbon (green = up, red = down)
    6. BMSB — Bull Market Support Band (21 EMA + 20 SMA)
    7. Regime Box — Background color (green = bullish, red = bearish)
    8. Supply/Demand Zones — Key levels with ⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐ ratings
    9. RCS Patterns — 16 candlestick patterns (see below)
    10. Meta Tools — Integration layer, coordinates all systems

    RCS Candlestick Pattern Library (16 Patterns)¶

    Bullish Patterns:
    HAM (Hammer), IH (Inverted Hammer), BUE (Bull Engulfing), BUH (Bull Harami), R3M (Rising Three Methods), 3WS (Three White Soldiers), MS (Morning Star), TWB (Tweezer Bottom)

    Bearish Patterns:
    HGM (Hanging Man), SS (Shooting Star), BEE (Bear Engulfing), BEH (Bear Harami), F3M (Falling Three Methods), 3BC (Three Black Crows), ES (Evening Star), TWT (Tweezer Top)

    📍 Janus Atlas — Levels System¶

    Comprehensive levels system with 60+ automatic level types.

    Timeframe Levels¶

    Daily: dH, dL, dO, dC, dMid (High, Low, Open, Close, Midpoint)
    Weekly: wH, wL, wO, wC, wMid
    Monthly: mH, mL, mO, mC, mMid
    Quarterly: QH, QL, QO, QC
    Yearly: YH, YL, YO, YC, YMid

    Session Levels¶

    Asian Session: Tokyo trading hours H/L/O/C
    European Session: London trading hours H/L/O/C
    North American Session: New York trading hours H/L/O/C

    VWAP Levels¶

    Current Period: dVWAP, wVWAP, mVWAP, qVWAP, yVWAP
    Previous Period: pdVWAP, pwVWAP, pmVWAP, etc.
    Standard Deviation Bands: ±1σ, ±2σ (overbought/oversold zones)

    Volume Profile¶

    POC (Point of Control): Price with highest traded volume — magnetic effect
    VAH (Value Area High): Top of 70% value area
    VAL (Value Area Low): Bottom of 70% value area
    nPOC (Naked POC): Previous POC levels not yet touched — unfilled magnets
    Timeframes: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly

    Market Structure¶

    HH: Higher High — uptrend continuation
    HL: Higher Low — uptrend pullback
    LH: Lower High — downtrend continuation
    LL: Lower Low — downtrend extension
    BOS: Break of Structure — trend continuation signal
    CHoCH: Change of Character — potential trend reversal

    Opening Range¶

    orH: Opening Range High
    orL: Opening Range Low
    orMid: Opening Range Midpoint
    Duration: Configurable 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes

    Killzones¶

    Visual: Background shading during high-probability windows
    Asian: 20:00-00:00 ET
    London Open: 02:00-05:00 ET
    NY Open: 07:00-10:00 ET
    London Close: 10:00-12:00 ET
    Custom: User-defined sessions

    Gap Levels¶

    gapH: Gap High (top of gap)
    gapL: Gap Low (bottom of gap)
    Behavior: Auto-clears when gap is filled — gaps act as magnets

    Fair Value Gaps (FVG)¶

    Bullish FVG: Gap up imbalance — demand zone, price often returns to fill
    Bearish FVG: Gap down imbalance — supply zone, price often returns to fill

    Fibonacci Levels¶

    Levels: 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, 0.886
    Anchors: 20 options including Previous Year H/L
    Sets: 2 independent Fib Sets can display simultaneously

    Confluence Zones¶

    Definition: Auto-detected areas where 2+ levels cluster together
    Significance: Higher probability S/R than individual levels

    📡 Augury Grid — Multi-Symbol Screener¶

    Real-time scanner displaying MACD histogram crossovers with confluence filtering across multiple assets.

    Grid Capacity¶

    Symbols: Monitor up to 8 tickers simultaneously
    Assets: Stocks, crypto, forex, futures — any combination

    Signal Detection¶

    ↑ Bullish: MACD histogram crosses above zero with confluence
    ↓ Bearish: MACD histogram crosses below zero with confluence
    — Neutral: No MACD crossover or insufficient confluence

    Quality Score (0-100)¶

    Scoring Factors:
    • ADX Trend (+10) — Trend strength ≥20
    • RSI Mid-Range (+10) — Not extreme (40-60)
    • Volume Spike (+15) — ≥1.3× average
    • Multi-TF 🔗 (+15) — 2 TFs agree
    • Multi-TF 🔗🔗 (+30) — 3 TFs agree

    Star Ratings: ★★★ ≥95 pts, ★★ ≥85, ★ ≥70, no stars <70

    Grid Display Elements¶

    Per Row: #, Sym, TF, Bias (Bull ★★★ 🔗🔗), Age, Entry, SL, TP, P&L
    Multi-TF Confluence: 🔗 = 2 TFs agree, 🔗🔗 = 3 TFs agree

    ⚡ Quick Reference¶

    Signal Colors At-a-Glance

    Pentarch: 🟣 TD → 🩵 IGN → 🟡 WRN → 🟠 CAP → 🔴 BDN

    Plutus Flow: ⚪ White = Breach (warning) | 🟡 Yellow = Exit (action) | 🟢/🔴 = Cross
