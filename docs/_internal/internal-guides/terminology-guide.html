3. Banned Terminology

The following terms must never be used in any Signal Pilot content:

NEVER Use These Terms

  • "Institutional" (in any context)
  • "Institutional flow"
  • "Institutional activity"
  • "Smart money"
  • "Whale activity"
  • "Big players"
  • "Professional traders" (when implying detection)

Use These Instead

  • "Volume flow"
  • "Regime detection"
  • "Accumulation/Distribution"
  • "Volume analysis"
  • "Statistical volume patterns"
  • "Volume footprints"
  • "Market regime"
Why? Terms like "institutional" and "smart money" imply the ability to detect specific market participants, which is misleading. Our indicators analyze volume patterns statistically - they cannot identify who is trading.
