10. The Four Numbers You Fix Before the First Trade

Lesson 84 prices six habits one at a time and finds that two of them together take the share of your accepted rules that is real from 43.2 per cent to 6.0. Every one of the six is defeated by writing a number down before you start rather than deciding it on the day. Module 11 ranks that against the other three freedoms the course did not have and it comes first, worth a factor of 7.16, and it costs nothing.

NumberMineWhere it comes from
Horizon — the trade count at which I will judge this, decided now trades Lesson 65. Judging after every trade instead takes a dead system’s pass rate from 5.05 per cent to 24.25.
Depth — the drawdown I will sit through without switching it off R Lesson 67. An eight-R line switches off 59.5 per cent of the systems that genuinely work.
Settings searched — how many variants I tried before choosing this one Lesson 64. Twenty configurations already cost 70 per cent of a single test’s bar; 15,000 over 156 trades manufacture 0.317 of an R a trade before the market has done anything.
Position limit — the most I will hold at once positions Lesson 75. Three per cent of heat over two per cent a trade permits 1.5 positions, which is one.

Filled in before the first trade, these four are a decision made once. Filled in afterwards, they are a description of what you already did.
