# Deletion Test Worksheet

*Signal Pilot Education — companion to Lesson 95: The Book on One Card.*

A book of rules is easy to admire and hard to justify. The test is not whether the book beats one of its members; it is whether every member earns its place. On the lesson's four-rule book, three of the four deletions improve it. This worksheet runs the same test on yours.

> **Educational only.** Trading involves substantial risk of loss. Not financial
> advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Every number in
> this worksheet is one you measure on your own record; the figures in the
> right-hand column are what the lesson measured on its own book, and they are
> there to be disagreed with rather than copied.

## 4. Write the sentence

> After deleting ____ , the book ____ . The member that earns its place least
> is ____ , and the reason I am still running it is ____ .

If the last blank is hard to fill in, that is the finding.

## What you are holding when this is filled in

One count and one comparison. The count is how many of your rules the book is
better off without; the comparison is whether the book beats the best thing in
it. Neither is an opinion once the card is filled in.
