Pro

💡 Key Insights
Confluence: Best trades happen when Fib levels align with other support/resistance zones. Multiple confirmations = higher probability.
🎯 Think Zones
Not lines: Price rarely bounces at exact numbers. Watch 10-20 pips around each level as a trading zone.
📊 Volume Check
Confirm strength: Look for decreased volume on the pullback. Fib levels work best in trending, not choppy, markets.
