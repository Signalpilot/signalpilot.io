The Lighthouse

"A beacon in the void. All eyes are drawn."
One cell becomes the undeniable focal point. Bright center radiates outward, dimming as it reaches the edges. The surrounding cells exist only to frame the light.
Why it works: Human eyes naturally seek the brightest point. Creates instant hierarchy.
Best for: Featured content, announcements, hero posts, product launches.
Execution: Radial gradient from white center, exponential falloff to near-black.
