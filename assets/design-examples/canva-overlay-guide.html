Canva Steps
1

Keep your starfield as the background

This stays exactly as it is. Don't change anything.

2

Add a rectangle (full size of slide)

Elements → Shapes → Square. Drag to cover the entire slide.

3

Set rectangle fill to gradient

Click the rectangle → Fill color → Gradient. Use your category color at top and bottom, transparent in middle.

4

Lower the transparency

Click rectangle → Transparency (top right) → Set to 10-15%

5

Optional: Add a thin color bar

For Education: Add a thin rectangle at top, fill with #00d4ff, height ~3-4px.
For Docs: Add thin rectangle on left side, fill with #94a3b8, width ~3-4px.

6

Send overlay to back (but above starfield)

Right-click rectangle → Layer → Send backward. It should be between starfield and your text/content.
