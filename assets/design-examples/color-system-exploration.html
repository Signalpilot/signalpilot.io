Approach D Cyan Spine — Center Dominant

Left and right columns are very muted. Center (education) is BRIGHT cyan. Your eye naturally follows the learning spine down the grid.

My Recommendation

I lean toward Approach B or D.

B if you want balanced visual rivers with clear column identity.

D if you want education (your funnel) to dominate and draw the eye.

But this is your brand. Which feels right to you?
